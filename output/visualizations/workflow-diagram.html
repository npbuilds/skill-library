How the bridge works

  • Claude Desktop starts server.py automatically when you open the app
  • Reads files from disk using relative paths — no database, no internet
  • New skills appear instantly — no restart, no deploy
  • Resolves nested hierarchy paths (skills/design/visual-communication/color-theory/...)
  • Everything stays local and private on your machine

What happens when you ask "help me pick colors for a dashboard"

  • Instructions kick in: Claude checks the library first
  • Claude calls: search_skills("color") → finds color-theory
  • Routes through hierarchy: design-orchestrator → visual-communication director
  • Director decides: load color-theory + visual-perception (perception constrains color)
  • Claude answers: using your curated methodology, not generic knowledge

🔑 The System is a Loop, Not a Pyramid

You build skills in Claude Code → they're saved as files on your Mac. The MCP server reads those files → Claude Desktop uses your methodology. The meta observer watches everything → spots gaps like "6 agents have no backing skills" and recommends what to build next. You decide → build the next skill → the loop continues.

Nobody sits "on top." You're the human who decides. The meta observer is your Chief of Staff who brings reports. The orchestrator is the Dean who manages a domain. The director is the Department Head who routes within a specialty. The knowledge skills are the Professors with deep expertise. And any new skill you build in any domain is instantly available everywhere.
