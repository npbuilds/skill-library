1

Run the export script

One command transforms your nested skill hierarchy into a flat Obsidian vault. Each SKILL.md gets enriched with wikilinks and YAML tags.

Terminal
$ bash scripts/export-obsidian.sh
Exporting skill library to Obsidian vault: exports/obsidian-vault
  Exported 95 of 95 skills
  Generated 9 domain MOCs
  Generated root index
  Copied .obsidian config

Done! Open 'exports/obsidian-vault' as an Obsidian vault.
  Skills: 95   MOCs: 9   Index: _Index.md

What happens under the hood

  • skills/design/visual/color-theory/SKILL.md → color-theory.md (flattened)
  • YAML frontmatter gets tags: [design, knowledge] injected
  • A ## Relationships section is appended with [[wikilinks]] to related skills
  • 9 domain MOC files and an _Index.md are auto-generated

Browse domains via MOCs (Maps of Content)

Each domain has a generated MOC that groups skills by type (orchestrator, director, knowledge, action). Quick way to survey what you have and spot gaps.

What This Gives You

🕸️

See the whole network

Graph view reveals clusters, orphaned skills with no connections, and cross-domain bridges you didn't know existed.

🔗

Follow the links

Click [[wikilinks]] to jump between skills. Backlinks show you everything that depends on the skill you're reading.

🔍

Spot gaps fast

MOCs group skills by type. If a domain has no orchestrator or only knowledge skills with no actions, that's a gap to fill.

🛡️

Safe & read-only

The vault is a disposable export. Edit your source SKILL.md files, re-run the script, and the vault regenerates fresh.
