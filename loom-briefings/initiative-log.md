## The Loom Product Domain

**Thesis:** An AI-native product orchestrator that cultivates intelligence products by weaving cross-domain capabilities into coherent surfaces will produce better products than traditional PM applied to AI.
**State:** germinating
**Domains involved:** all (The Loom orchestrates all domain orchestrators)
**Seeded:** 2026-04-13
**Last transition:** seed → germinating on 2026-04-13

### Transitions
- 2026-04-13: seed → germinating — Phase 1 complete (orchestrator + references + synthesize director). Conditions being designed through Phases 2-4.

### Key Learnings
- The paradigm shift from traditional PM to AI-native thinking was the most important design decision
- Organic lifecycle metaphor (seed → compost) resonates more than industrial metaphor (build → ship → kill)

### Kill Criteria
- If after 3 months the paradigm produces no actionable product decisions that differ from what traditional PM would produce
- If the organic lifecycle adds confusion without adding insight

### Open Questions
- How should The Loom interact with products that have external users vs. personal use only?
- What's the right cadence for the weekly product synthesis?
