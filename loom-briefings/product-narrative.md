## The Story So Far

The skill library began as a structured knowledge system — domains of expertise organized into orchestrators, directors, and knowledge skills. It grew organically, domain by domain: wine, investing, writing, design, game theory, worldbuilding, data science, philosophy.

In April 2026, a new layer emerged: **The Loom** — a product orchestrator that sits above all domains and asks "what should we build with all this intelligence?" The Loom introduced an AI-native product design paradigm: products aren't manufactured, they're cultivated. Capabilities aren't features, they're living surfaces.

The system is now self-aware enough to plan its own product evolution.

## Decision Log

### Decision: AI-Native Paradigm for The Loom
**Date:** 2026-04-13
**Context:** Initial Loom v1 used traditional PM lifecycle. Felt wrong for intelligence products.
**Options considered:** (1) Traditional PM with AI flavor, (2) Fully novel AI-native paradigm, (3) Hybrid
**Chose:** Fully novel AI-native paradigm
**Reasoning:** The skill library itself is already a living system. Products built from it should be designed as living systems, not manufactured artifacts.
**What would prove this wrong:** If the organic lifecycle (seed → compost) produces less useful products than a traditional feature roadmap would.
