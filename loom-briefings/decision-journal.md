## 2026-04-13: AI-Native Product Paradigm

**Context:** Building a master product orchestrator (The Loom) for the skill library. Initial v1 used traditional PM lifecycle (discovery, strategy, shaping, launch, growth).

**Options considered:**
1. **Traditional PM with AI flavor** — Keep the standard lifecycle, add AI-specific considerations
2. **Fully novel AI-native paradigm** — Redesign from scratch around cultivating intelligence systems
3. **Hybrid** — Traditional lifecycle but with organic language and emergence awareness

**Chose:** Option 2 — Fully novel AI-native paradigm

**Reasoning:**
- The skill library is already a living system — it grows, connects, evolves
- Products built from living intelligence should be designed as living systems
- Traditional PM optimizes for shipping discrete features; intelligence products need to optimize for capability evolution
- The organic lifecycle (seed → compost) better captures what actually happens with AI products

**What would prove this wrong:**
- If the paradigm produces beautiful-sounding plans that never translate to real product decisions
- If "cultivation" becomes an excuse for not shipping
- If users/collaborators can't understand the framework (too abstract)
