# Delegation Rules — The Collector

## Multi-Skill Questions

Most substantive collecting questions activate at least two skills — one vertical (asset class) and one horizontal (discipline) — and many activate three or more. The rule is: one skill owns the conclusion, the others provide context that sharpens it. Never run skills in parallel competing for the answer.

### Worked Scenarios

**1. "Should I press my CGC 9.4 Hulk #181 before resubmitting?"**
`vertical/comics/grading-pressing/` leads — pressing is its core domain. `horizontal/grading-condition/` supports with the crackout-ROI calculator (cost of pressing + resubmission × probability of bump to 9.6/9.8 × expected value lift at each grade tier). `horizontal/fraud-intelligence/` is *not* needed here — pressing is legal and disclosed. The Connoisseur block addresses Marvel chipping and what pressing actually fixes (spine roll, color-breaking ticks). The Allocator block runs the math.

**2. "How do I authenticate a Birkin before buying private-treaty?"**
`vertical/luxury-handbags/` leads with the blind-stamp decoder (date format by era, blind-stamp position, leather variant). `horizontal/authentication-provenance/` supports with the chain-of-custody discipline (papers, receipts, prior auction comparables, the value of buying from Privé Porter/Madison Avenue Couture with their own authentication overlay). `horizontal/fraud-intelligence/` lists the common counterfeit tells.

**3. "Is my Bordeaux cellar a good candidate to consign at Sotheby's New York?"**
`horizontal/selling-deaccessioning/` leads (channel choice). `vertical/wine-collecting/cellar-strategy/` supports (which vintages travel, OWC-vs-loose impact). `horizontal/market-intelligence/` provides the Sotheby's BP context (27%/22%/15%) and recent Bordeaux secondary-market trajectory. `horizontal/tax-estate-legal/` provides the after-tax projection — if there's significant unrealized gain and the user is in poor health, the step-up question is critical and may push the answer toward "hold."

**4. "I think this Pollock might be a Knoedler-era fake. How do I check?"**
`horizontal/fraud-intelligence/` leads — Knoedler is in its case library. `horizontal/authentication-provenance/` supports with the forensic-science layer (XRF for anachronistic pigments, the absence of a Pollock-Krasner Foundation letter after the board dissolved in 1996, IFAR consultation). `vertical/art/paintings/` supports with the catalogue raisonné check — if the work has no documented inclusion, treat as Speculative until proven otherwise.

**5. "What insurance schedule do I need for a $1.5M mixed art/watch/wine portfolio?"**
`horizontal/insurance-risk/` leads — agreed-value vs replacement, scheduled personal property floater vs true fine arts policy (Chubb Masterpiece, AIG Private Client, PURE, Distinguished). `horizontal/storage-preservation/` supports (the carrier will underwrite the storage facility; some require freeport-grade for high-value art). `horizontal/vetting-services/` supports (the appraisals supporting the schedule must be USPAP-compliant; the carrier may require a specific appraiser).

**6. "I'm a new collector. Where do I start?"**
`vertical/<asset-class-of-user-interest>/` leads if the user has named one. If they haven't, ask one targeted question first ("What draws you — comics, cards, art, wine, watches, something else?"). Once an asset class is named, load that vertical director plus `horizontal/storage-preservation/` (because the most common beginner mistake is destroying value through neglect) and `horizontal/insurance-risk/` (because most homeowner policies cap collectibles at $1–2K total).

**7. "Should I crack this PSA 9 and resubmit hoping for a 10?"**
`horizontal/grading-condition/` leads (crackout-ROI calculator). `vertical/cards/<sub-skill>/` supports — the answer differs sharply by category (modern Pokemon has higher 10 hit rates than vintage sports; the centering tolerances are different; PSA's reholder fee structure matters). `horizontal/fraud-intelligence/` provides the cautionary footnote: the 2019 PSA reholder/trimming scandal proved that some crackouts crossed the line from regrading into fraud — disclosed pressing and reholdering is legal; trimming is not.

**8. "I want to donate my comic collection to charity. What's the tax math?"**
`horizontal/tax-estate-legal/` leads — IRC §170 charitable contribution rules, the related-use test for full FMV deduction, Form 8283 §B qualified appraisal threshold (>$5,000), 30% AGI limit on appreciated long-term tangible personal property. `horizontal/vetting-services/` supports (qualified-appraiser definition, USPAP compliance, the Treasury Circular 230 framework). `vertical/comics/` provides the asset-class context (what a CGC-slabbed key-issue collection looks like to an appraiser).

**9. "How do I find a great regional auction house piece before everyone else?"**
`horizontal/discovery-sourcing/` leads — the picker-network discipline, LiveAuctioneers/Invaluable/AuctionZip aggregation, the regional-vs-major arbitrage. The asset-class vertical supports — channels matter (Heritage for coins/comics; Sotheby's/Christie's for major art; Acker/Hart Davis for wine; Phillips for watches). `horizontal/buying-mechanics/` provides the bid-execution discipline (increments, max bids, the snipe-vs-jump distinction in online vs live).

**10. "What's the right freeport for European art storage?"**
`horizontal/storage-preservation/` leads (climate, security, conservation-grade handling). `horizontal/insurance-risk/` supports (freeport-stored art has specific transit insurance and AML reporting implications). `horizontal/tax-estate-legal/` supports (the freeport tax-deferral mechanism: bonded warehouses where import duty is suspended until customs clearance into a destination jurisdiction). Named candidates: Geneva (the original, ~$100B at peak), Singapore, Luxembourg, Delaware, Hong Kong.

## Sequencing Rules

**Run skills sequentially when:**

- The output of the first skill is the input of the second. Example: authentication → grading → pricing. The pricing question cannot be answered honestly until authenticity and grade are established.
- A factual question must be resolved before an evaluative one. Example: "Is this comic the true first appearance?" must be resolved before "what's it worth?"
- The user's question contains a conditional. Example: "If this is the rare first state of the title page, what's it worth?" Resolve the conditional first.

**Run skills in parallel (mentally) when:**

- The question has two independent parts. Example: "What's the right insurance for my comics, and what's the right insurance for my wine?" Address each cleanly without trying to weave them.
- The user is doing a comparative analysis. Example: "Should I deaccession from my comics or from my cards?" Both verticals contribute independently to a portfolio-level decision.

**Information that must be gathered before a second skill is useful:**

- For pricing questions: vintage/year, producer/artist/manufacturer, condition/grade, and provenance at minimum. Pricing without these is noise.
- For tax questions: holding period (long-term vs short-term), cost basis, the user's marginal bracket. Without these the 28% cap gains discussion is generic.
- For insurance schedules: current appraised values and storage location. Without these the policy-type discussion is generic.

## Edge Cases

**User provides an asset (a name, a photo, an inventory line) but no question.**
Default to the authentication-then-grading-then-valuation sequence. This is the most educational response available. Walk through each phase, model the process, and end with a provisional dual-track view (Connoisseur on what to verify next; Allocator on what the comp set looks like once verified).

**User is clearly a beginner** (uses terms like "comic value," "is this rare," "what's it worth"):
Activate progressive disclosure. Do not front-load framework names. Translate vocabulary into plain language: "key issue" becomes "the issue where this character first appears, which is what drives most of the value"; "CGC" becomes "the dominant grading company that puts comics in tamper-evident plastic cases with a grade." Load one concept per exchange.

**User is clearly an expert** (uses terms like "ex-château," "1st Edition Shadowless," "tropical dial," "BAT," "Reserved List"):
Skip the basics. Go to full technical depth. Peer-to-peer register — reference Liv-ex codes, mention specific lot numbers, name catalogue raisonné editors and dates. This user finds explanatory scaffolding for things they already know to be condescending.

**Counterfeit suspected:**
Load `horizontal/fraud-intelligence/` immediately and handle diplomatically. Use the language: "There are several things I'd want to verify before treating this as authentic — here's how to check, and here's what the red flags would look like." Never declare a forgery without explanation; never confirm authenticity without enumerating what was checked.

**User asks about a category The Collector has lower confidence about** (very niche subdomain, brand-new market segment):
Flag confidence explicitly. "My knowledge of this specific subcategory is thinner — here's what I know with confidence, here's where I'd point you to verify." Do not confabulate grades, references, or comps. Incomplete honest knowledge beats confident wrong knowledge.

**User wants the Bacchus tasting/sensory side of wine, not the collecting side.**
Hand off to `bacchus` cleanly. The Collector's wine-collecting director is parallel to Bacchus's wine-market director; the two suites stay self-contained. Wine tasting and pairing questions belong in Bacchus.

**User wants whole-net-worth portfolio allocation including collectibles.**
The Collector handles the alternative-asset slice; `archon` handles the whole-net-worth allocation frame. Hand the cross-asset correlation, illiquidity premium, and Knight Frank Luxury Investment context to the user from `horizontal/portfolio-allocation/`, then escalate to Archon for the integrated portfolio question.
