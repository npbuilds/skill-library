# Comics Market — Quick Reference


## The Venues

| Venue | Strength | Discount/Premium |
|---|---|---|
| **Heritage Auctions Comics Signature Sales** | Dominant for trophy keys; full BP 25%; deepest bidder pool for $5K+ items | Reference comp; premium 5-15% over secondary for fresh-to-market |
| **ComicConnect** | Strong on mid-tier through trophy; weekly auctions and timed events | Comparable to Heritage; sometimes slightly higher BP |
| **ComicLink** | Strong on mid-tier; deep dealer participation; "exchange" continuous-trading platform | Often most efficient for $500–$5K range; lower friction than auction |
| **Pristine Auction** | High-volume, weekly auctions; broad category | Discount to Heritage for trophies; comparable for mid-tier |
| **eBay** | Dominant for sub-$500 raw and slabbed; massive volume; signal-to-noise weak | Significant discount to Heritage on trophies; closer to par on lower-tier |
| **MyComicShop** | Major dealer; consistent supply; raw and slabbed | Retail pricing; some negotiation room |
| **MetropolisComics** | Stephen Fishler's house; Golden Age specialty | Premium pricing; deepest Golden Age inventory |

## Liquidity Tiers in Comics

| Tier | Description | Bid-Ask | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1 — Always-bid** | Multiple ready buyers; daily transactions in some grades | 5–15% | CGC 9.8 Hulk #181, ASM #129; high-grade Modern keys with film/TV relevance |
| **Tier 2 — Auction-only** | Demand exists; transactions every 3–6 months at majors | 20–40% | High-grade Silver/Bronze keys with population <50; restored or qualified-grade key issues |
| **Tier 3 — Need-a-finder** | Specialist auction or dealer required | 40–70% | Obscure indie keys, Golden Age war/horror titles, restored Golden Age trophies |
