# Mtg Collecting — Quick Reference


## Venues

| Venue | Strength |
|---|---|
| **TCGplayer** | Dominant for singles trading; reliable pricing data; the order-book equivalent for MTG |
| **Star City Games** | Major retailer; tournament infrastructure; pricing reference |
| **Card Kingdom** | Major retailer; high-end singles |
| **eBay** | Trophy cards (Power Nine, Reserved List staples); raw and slabbed |
| **Cardmarket** | European-domestic equivalent of TCGplayer |
| **MTG Stocks** | Pricing aggregator and trend analysis |
| **Heritage / PWCC** | High-end trophy slabs (Alpha Black Lotus, etc.) |
