# Tcg Other — Quick Reference

| Card | Why It Matters |
|---|---|
| **Tyler the Great Warrior** (2005) | Make-A-Wish unique card; 1 known; ~$300K+ at last public knowledge |
| **Tournament Black Luster Soldier** (1999) | 7 known; trophy from the original Japanese tournament; multi-hundred-thousand at recent sales |
| **Skuna, the Leonine Rakan** (2018) | 1 known; Make-A-Wish |
| **Stardust Dragon "Tournament Black"** | Limited tournament-prize printings |

## Venues

| Venue | Strength |
|---|---|
| **TCGplayer** | Dominant for most TCG singles; Yu-Gi-Oh, Lorcana, Flesh and Blood, smaller TCGs |
| **eBay** | All TCGs; high-value singles especially |
| **Cardmarket** | European-domestic equivalent |
| **TCG-specific marketplaces** | Some TCGs have dedicated marketplaces (e.g., dedicated Flesh and Blood communities) |
| **Heritage / PWCC** | High-end Yu-Gi-Oh trophies and tournament prizes |
