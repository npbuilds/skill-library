# Sports Cards — Quick Reference


## Grading Dynamics

| Grader | Strength | Weakness |
|---|---|---|
| **PSA** | Liquidity king; deepest pop report; market premium | Conservative on centering; opaque (no subgrades) |
| **BGS (Beckett)** | Subgrades published; "Black Label" / "Pristine" tier | Lower market premium than PSA at same numeric grade; weaker liquidity |
| **SGC** | Preferred for raw vintage (pre-1970); clean slab design | Smaller pop on modern; some discount to PSA at modern |
| **CGC** | Newer entrant; clean slabs; competitive on Pokémon and entering sports | Limited track record on sports; market premium to PSA still developing |

## Venues

| Venue | Strength |
|---|---|
| **Heritage Auctions** | Trophy cards (T206 Wagner, vintage Mantle, modern RC patches); deepest bidder pool for $5K+ |
| **Goldin** | Major card auctions; Ken Goldin's house; weekly auctions; strong on modern |
| **Fanatics Collect** (formerly PWCC Marketplace) | Premier dealer-marketplace; major auction events; pop-report integration |
| **PWCC** | Continues as the index brand; auction house function transitioned to Fanatics Collect |
| **eBay** | Dominant for sub-$5K; massive volume; signal-to-noise weak |
| **WhatNot** | Live-streaming auction format; strong on cards and Pokémon |
| **CardLadder** | Index pricing layer; tracks comps |
