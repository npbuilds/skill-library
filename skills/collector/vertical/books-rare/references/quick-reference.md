# Books Rare — Quick Reference


## The Trade Bodies

| Body | Coverage | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **ABAA** (Antiquarian Booksellers' Association of America) | US | Founded 1949; ~440 member dealers; rigorous vetting; ABAA member listing on a dealer's site signals trade reputation |
| **ILAB** (International League of Antiquarian Booksellers) | International | Umbrella body for national associations |
| **PBFA** (Provincial Booksellers Fairs Association) | UK | Smaller-dealer focus; runs book fairs |
| **SLAR** (Société des Libraires de l'Ancien Régime) | France | French national association |

## Venues

| Venue | Strength |
|---|---|
| **Sotheby's, Christie's** | Trophy first editions; iconic association copies; manuscripts |
| **Bonhams** | Strong on 20th-century moderns |
| **Heritage Auctions** | Heritage Rare Books & Manuscripts Signature Sales; broad inventory |
| **Swann Galleries** | New York; specialty in 20th-century books, fine printing, photographs |
| **Bauman Rare Books** | Major dealer; bricks-and-mortar in DC, NY, Las Vegas; high-end inventory |
| **Peter Harrington** | UK; major dealer; high-end inventory; deep modernist focus |
| **Type Punch Matrix** | DC-based; modern dealer-press; younger collector engagement |
| **AbeBooks** | Aggregator for ABAA and other dealers; online inventory |
| **ABAA.org** | Direct dealer search across ABAA members |
