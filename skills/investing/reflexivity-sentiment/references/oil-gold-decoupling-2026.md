## Application to Reflexivity Theory

This episode illustrates a core Soros principle: **two assets can be at different phases of the boom-bust cycle simultaneously, even when driven by the same underlying event.**

Gold was in Phase 3-4 (Acceleration → Test) when the Iran crisis began, and has now entered Phase 6 because it ran ahead of the fundamentals. Oil was in Phase 1-2 (Incipient trend → Reinforcement) when the crisis began and is now in Phase 5.

When identifying reflexive opportunities, always ask: **what PHASE is each asset in, not just what is the fundamental?**
