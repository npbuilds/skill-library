# Case Study: Dual-Shock Stagflation — 2026 Hormuz + Tariff Episode

*Added by Archon briefing protocol — April 9, 2026*

## What Makes This Regime Unique

The 2026 stagflationary episode is a **compound shock** not seen in isolation in prior historical analogs. Two simultaneous supply/demand disruptions hit from different directions:

1. **Energy Supply Shock (Hormuz):** US/Israel strikes on Iran triggered effective closure of the Strait of Hormuz (20% of global oil supply). Brent crude crossed $100 on March 8 and hit ~$128 at peak. Oil up ~100% YTD by early April. IEA warned April 2026 would be "worse than March" in supply terms.

2. **Demand/Trade Policy Shock (Tariffs):** Trump administration's Section 122 tariffs (10% global baseline, higher for China and ~60 countries) took effect April 9, 2026 — following Supreme Court striking down the IEEPA tariff authority. ISM Services Prices soared to 70.7% (near Oct 2022 post-pandemic peak).

## The Policy Trap Dynamic

The compound nature creates a **policy trap** that didn't exist in prior analogs:
- **Can't cut:** Oil/tariff inflation running at 3%+ Core PCE and accelerating
- **Can't hike:** Atlanta Fed GDPNow turned negative Q1 2026; unemployment rising to 4.4%
- **Result:** Fed frozen at 3.64% while both inflation and recession risk rise simultaneously

*Key signal to watch:* Which shock resolves first determines the exit path.
- If Hormuz de-escalates first → inflation falls → Fed can cut → growth recovers
- If tariff negotiations begin first → demand uncertainty fades → Fed watches energy
- If neither resolves → 1970s-style prolonged stagflation with eventual policy capitulation
