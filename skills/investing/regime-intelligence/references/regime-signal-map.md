# Regime Signal Map — Quick Reference

## The Four Regimes

| Regime | Growth | Inflation | Best Assets | Worst Assets |
|--------|--------|-----------|-------------|--------------|
| Goldilocks | ↑ | ↓ | Equities (growth), IG credit, EM | Gold, commodities, cash |
| Reflation | ↑ | ↑ | Commodities, value equities, TIPS | Long bonds, growth stocks |
| Deflation/Recession | ↓ | ↓ | Long treasuries, quality equities | Cyclicals, HY credit, commodities |
| Stagflation | ↓ | ↑ | Gold, TIPS, cash, managed futures | Everything else |

## Regime Transition Checklist

Before calling a regime change, require ≥3 of 5 signals to confirm:
- [ ] Leading indicators (PMI, LEI) confirm the direction
- [ ] Market-implied signals (breakevens, credit spreads) confirm
- [ ] Hard data (claims, wages) confirm
- [ ] At least 2 consecutive months of data pointing same direction
- [ ] No dominant idiosyncratic factor distorting readings
