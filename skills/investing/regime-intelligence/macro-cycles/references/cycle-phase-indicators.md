# Business Cycle Phase Indicators — Quick Reference

## Phase Identification Matrix

| Indicator | Early Cycle | Mid Cycle | Late Cycle | Recession |
|-----------|-------------|-----------|------------|-----------|
| GDP growth | Accelerating | Steady (2-3%) | Decelerating | Negative |
| ISM Manufacturing | 50-55, rising | 52-57, stable | 50-55, falling | <50, falling |
| Unemployment | Falling fast | Falling slow | Stable/rising | Rising fast |
| Yield curve (10Y-2Y) | Steep (+150bps+) | Normalizing | Flat/inverted | Steepening (bull) |
| Credit spreads | Tightening rapidly | Stable/tight | Gradual widening | Blowing out |
| Inflation | Low, rising | Rising | High, peaking | Falling |
| Fed funds rate | Low, stable | Rising | Peak | Cutting |
| Corporate profits | Recovering | Growing | Slowing | Declining |

## Leading Indicators Scorecard

Rate each as Green (+1), Yellow (0), or Red (-1). Score 5+ = current phase confidence.

**Leading (3-6 month lead):**
- [ ] Yield curve slope
- [ ] ISM New Orders minus Inventories
- [ ] Building permits / housing starts
- [ ] Initial jobless claims (inverted)
- [ ] S&P 500 (inverted for recession signal)
- [ ] Conference Board LEI

**Coincident (current state):**
- [ ] Payroll employment
- [ ] Industrial production
- [ ] Real personal income ex-transfers
- [ ] Real manufacturing and trade sales

## Sector Positioning by Phase (summary)

| Phase | Overweight | Underweight |
|-------|-----------|-------------|
| Early | Financials, Discretionary, Industrials | Utilities, Staples |
| Mid | Technology, Industrials, Communication | Utilities, Materials |
| Late | Energy, Materials, Staples, Healthcare | Discretionary, Financials, Tech |
| Recession | Healthcare, Utilities, Staples | Financials, Industrials, Discretionary |

## Recession Probability Models

| Model | Signal | Accuracy |
|-------|--------|---------|
| Yield curve (2Y-10Y inverted) | Recession in 12-18 months | High (80%+, but long lead) |
| Sahm Rule (0.5% unemployment rise) | Recession NOW | Very high (near real-time) |
| Conference Board LEI (6 consecutive monthly declines) | Recession in 3-6 months | High |
| Credit spreads (HY >600 bps) | Recession or financial stress | High for financial recessions |
| PMI <47 for 3+ months | Manufacturing recession | High for manufacturing; mixed for overall |
