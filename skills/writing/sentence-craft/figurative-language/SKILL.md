# Figurative Language — The Art of Saying One Thing and Meaning Another

Figurative language is not decoration. It is the primary mechanism by which prose creates meaning that cannot be stated directly — emotional texture, thematic resonance, the feeling of understanding something unexplained.

Lakoff and Johnson's foundational insight: metaphor is not a mode of language but a mode of thought. We understand abstract domains by mapping them onto concrete, embodied experience. When a character describes relationships in economic terms ("investing" in people, "bankrupt" emotions), that isn't style — it's cognitive architecture. The metaphors betray the worldview.

## The Metaphor Spectrum

**Dead** → **Conventional** → **Original** → **Extended/Conceit**

- **Dead**: "Table leg." Invisible — neither helps nor hurts unless deliberately reactivated.
- **Conventional**: "Broken heart." Clarity without surprise. Workhorses of transparent prose.
- **Original**: Novel comparisons requiring actual thought. The first comparison that comes to mind is the cliché; the third or fourth is where originality lives.
- **Extended**: Sustained across sentences or paragraphs. Each elaboration must deepen meaning, not repeat it.

**Deployment principle**: Dead/conventional for pace. Original for emotional intensity. Extended for thematic architecture. Uniform original metaphors lose their power.

## Simile vs. Metaphor

Not "like vs. is" — different cognitive effects:

- **Metaphor** asserts identity, creates immediacy, compresses. Use for action, emotion-dense passages.
- **Simile** maintains the gap between tenor and vehicle. That gap creates productive strangeness, precision, contemplative distance. Use when the comparison's distance is the point.

## The Objective Correlative

Find the external object/situation that *is* the emotion — so giving the external facts immediately evokes the feeling. Objects gain meaning through context, not inherent symbolism. They accrue compound interest through reappearance. The craft-theoretical foundation of "show don't tell."

## Image Systems

A family of related images from a single conceptual domain (water, machinery, birds) deployed throughout a work for thematic coherence.

- Match to character environment
- Evolve the relationship over time
- Combine cultural and context-created associations
- Discover in revision, don't force in first drafts
- If it feels like a lesson, it's overdone

## Symbol and Allegory

**Organic symbol** emerges from narrative context. **Imposed symbol** forces meaning the story hasn't earned. Test: can you remove the symbol and still articulate the theme? If yes, it's ornamental.

Allegory works when the surface story is independently compelling. Fails when characters are concepts wearing costumes.

## Synesthesia

Describing one sense through another ("sharp taste," "velvety voice"). Creates vividness through cognitive disruption. Use for defamiliarization and altered consciousness. Overuse dulls the effect.

## AI Prose and Figurative Language

AI reaches for conventional metaphors. Original figurative language requires experiential grounding, willingness to risk failure, and thematic consciousness across thousands of words — the biggest differentiator between "AI-generated" and "authored" prose.

## Practitioners

| Author | Strategy | Effect |
|--------|----------|--------|
| Morrison | Metaphor as incantation — rhythmic, accumulating | Mythic register, oral tradition |
| McCarthy | Landscape as metaphor | Environment carries moral weight |
| VanderMeer | Biological metaphor | The "weird" through familiar biology |
| Woolf | Metaphor as consciousness | Interiority and figuration fuse |
| Nabokov | Metaphor as performance | The narrator's skill IS character |

## Relationship to Other Skills

- **Diction**: Literal word choice; figurative language is *associative* word choice.
- **Concrete Detail**: Provides sensory specificity that figures require. Vague metaphors fail; specific ones succeed.
- **Character Interiority**: The perception filter IS sustained figurative language.
- **Micro-Tension**: Original figures create tension through surprise; clichés kill it.
- **Style DNA**: Dimension 4 (Ornamentation) measures figurative density.
