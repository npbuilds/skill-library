## The Reds at a Glance

| Variety | Structure | Climate | Signature |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cabernet Sauvignon | High tannin, high acid, full body | Warm | Blackcurrant, pencil shavings |
| Merlot | Medium tannin, plush texture | Versatile | Plum, chocolate |
| Pinot Noir | Low tannin, high acid, light body | Cool only | Red cherry, earth, silk |
| Syrah/Shiraz | High tannin, full body | Cool = peppery, warm = jammy | Olive tapenade / blackberry |
| Nebbiolo | Very high tannin, high acid | Cool (Piedmont) | Tar, roses, dried cherry |
| Sangiovese | High acid, medium tannin | Tuscan warmth | Sour cherry, leather |
| Tempranillo | Medium tannin, low acid | Spanish plateau | Red berry, tobacco |
| Grenache | Low tannin, high alcohol | Hot and dry | Raspberry, garrigues |
| Zinfandel | High alcohol, jammy | California heat | Bramble, black pepper |

## The Whites at a Glance

| Variety | Acid | Body | Signature |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chardonnay | Medium | Medium–full | Neutral base, reflects terroir and oak |
| Sauvignon Blanc | High | Light–medium | Grassy, grapefruit, gooseberry |
| Riesling | Very high | Light | Petrol, green apple, lime; ages magnificently |
| Chenin Blanc | High | Variable | Quince, lanolin, honey; extreme versatility |
| Gewürztraminer | Low | Full | Lychee, rose petal, broad aromatic |
| Pinot Gris | Low–medium | Full | Peach, smoke, richness without drama |
