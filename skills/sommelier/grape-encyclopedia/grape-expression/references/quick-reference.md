# Grape Expression — Quick Reference


## What Changes Across These Four Regions

| Factor | Burgundy | Oregon | Central Otago | Sonoma Coast |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Primary soil type | Limestone/clay marl | Volcanic basalt | Schist | Sandstone/volcanic |
| Diurnal range | Low-medium | Medium | Very high | High (coastal) |
| Color depth | Pale-medium | Medium | Medium-deep | Medium |
| Acid | Very high | Medium-high | High (retained) | Medium-high |
| Tannin | Low-medium | Medium | Medium-higher | Low-medium |
| Fruit character | Red cherry/earth | Strawberry/plum | Dark cherry/mocha | Red fruit/brighter |
| Typical ABV | 12–13.5% | 13–14.5% | 13.5–14.5% | 13.5–15% |
| Aging potential | Very high | Medium-high | Medium-high | Medium |

## The Winemaking Variables in Chardonnay

| Winemaking Choice | Effect on Wine |
|---|---|
| New French oak | Toast, vanilla, cedar, spice; integrates with time |
| Full MLF | Butter, cream, lower acid (malic → lactic) |
| Blocked MLF | Crisper, more apple/citrus, higher perceived acid |
| Bâtonnage (lees stirring) | Richer texture, nutty notes, creamy mouthfeel |
| Wild yeast | More complexity, more risk, more site expression |
| No oak at all | Pure fruit and terroir; shows the grape unadorned |
| High alcohol harvest | Tropical fruit, lower acid, broader palate |
