# Cost Structure Patterns

Detailed patterns for designing magic system costs. The cost is the most important design decision — it determines who uses the system, how often, and what kind of conflicts emerge.

## Single-Cost Systems

Simple but effective. One clear price for one clear power.

| Pattern | Cost | Example | Narrative Strength |
|---------|------|---------|-------------------|
| **Fuel** | Consumes a material | Allomancy (metals) | Supply chain drama; economic pressure |
| **Fatigue** | Physical exhaustion | Sympathy (body heat) | Human limits create tension |
| **Time** | Takes hours/days to prepare | Ritual magic | Planning vs. urgency trade-offs |
| **Knowledge** | Requires understanding to use | Naming (Kingkiller) | Learning IS the power progression |

## Compound-Cost Systems

Two or more costs layered. These create richer strategic decisions.

| Pattern | Costs | Example | Narrative Strength |
|---------|-------|---------|-------------------|
| **Fuel + Risk** | Material consumed + chance of catastrophe | Hemalurgy (metals + death/corruption) | Resource management meets moral stakes |
| **Oath + Power** | Binding vow limits behavior + grants ability | Binding Vows (JJK) | The stronger the oath, the stronger the power; freedom vs. power |
| **Training + Toll** | Years of study + physical/mental degradation | Many martial arts systems | Experience matters; masters are damaged |
| **Access + Addiction** | Must be near a source + dependency grows | Spice (Dune) | Creates literal dependency; withdrawal as vulnerability |

## Escalating-Cost Systems

The more you use it, the higher the cost. Creates natural narrative arcs.

```
First use:  trivial cost     → "This is easy. Why doesn't everyone do this?"
Tenth use:  noticeable cost  → "I'm starting to feel the effects."
Hundredth:  severe cost      → "I'm losing more than I'm gaining."
Final use:  catastrophic     → "This will be the last time."
```

This pattern naturally creates character arcs — the early wonder, the growing dependency, the crisis, the final sacrifice.

## Hidden-Cost Systems

The user doesn't know the full cost until it's too late. Narratively powerful but must be foreshadowed.

- **Delayed**: Effects don't appear until much later (cancerous magic, shortened lifespan)
- **Displaced**: Someone else pays the cost (unwitting sacrifice, generational debt)
- **Cumulative**: Each use is fine; the total is devastating (memory erosion, personality change)
- **Karmic**: The universe balances the books in unexpected ways (equivalent exchange, monkey's paw)

## Cost and Society

The cost structure shapes institutions:

| If the cost is... | Society creates... |
|-------------------|--------------------|
| Material | Mining guilds, trade monopolies, smugglers |
| Physical | Hospitals, retirement systems for burned-out users |
| Knowledge | Academies, libraries, teacher-student traditions |
| Moral | Ethics boards, religious prohibitions, secret police |
| Social | Oaths of service, contracts, feudal obligations |
| Hidden | Conspiracy, cover-ups, whistleblowers |
