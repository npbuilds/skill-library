# Geography Ecology — Quick Reference


## Quick Reference

| Climate | Civilization Character | Narrative Mood |
|---------|----------------------|----------------|
| Tropical / lush | Abundance, stratification, decay, rebirth cycles | Fecund, overwhelming, entropy |
| Arid / desert | Efficiency, fundamentalism, nomadism, oasis politics | Stark, unforgiving, sacred |
| Temperate | Balance, agriculture, cities, institutional complexity | Measured, historic, layered |
| Arctic / tundra | Scarcity, community bonds, oral tradition, survival focus | Endurance, isolation, purity |
| Volcanic / unstable | Fatalism, spiritual intensity, innovation born of necessity | Urgency, impermanence, power |

## Quick Reference

| Scale | Travel Time (pre-industrial) | Political Implication |
|-------|------------------------------|----------------------|
| City-state | Hours | Direct governance, face-to-face politics |
| Region | Days to weeks | Governor systems, local autonomy |
| Continent | Months | Empires fragment, regional cultures diverge |
| Global | Years | No true central authority, trade empires |
