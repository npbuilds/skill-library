# Stress-Tests — the "And Then What?" detectors

The soundness rubric checks that a system *propagates*; these detectors check the propagation *one iteration deeper* — the second-order consequences a **forward-built** world (axiom → implication) tends to miss. This is the `extrapolation-engine` turned on the *cracks*, not just the build. Most real worldbuilding gaps are one of these.

Each is a **find-the-gap** test: run it against the system, and if the gap is real, cite the axiom that should have propagated and FAIL the mapped dimension. As always, **steelman first** — an answered gap (the world has a stated reason) is a PASS, even if the reason is unusual.

> These were derived by running them by hand on a real, strong world (the "Bloom" system). Every one of them found a genuine pressure point in a top-decile world-bible — which is exactly why a *forward*-built world needs a *backward* stress pass.

---

## 1. Enforcement physics → FAILs **Cost is real** / **Plausibility**

| Gap to find | Why it fails | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| A rule is "enforced" but the *mechanism* is unstated ("a binding, quasi-magically enforced pact") | The enforcement is the load-bearing thing that makes the economy/monopoly work; if it's hand-waved, you can't tell if the system is iron or paper | State the physics: is the violation **impossible** (a hard lock) or **possible-but-detected/punished** (policing)? The two imply very different black markets |
| A scarce resource is "controlled" but nothing physically stops bypass | Monopoly asserted, not mechanized | Name what stops an unlicensed/unauthorized actor — detection, physics, or force |

## 2. Monopoly on violence → FAILs **Propagation** (society) / **Plausibility**

| Gap to find | Why it fails | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Economic/political power is mapped exhaustively but **coercive** power is absent — who arrests, polices, compels? | A society with no answer to "who holds legitimate force" can't actually function; the order rests on nothing | Specify the coercion layer (state, guild enforcers, a watch) or explicitly justify its absence |
| An arbiter "cannot compel" and there's no backstop | The whole order rests on goodwill | Name the real sanction (economic, reputational, force) and who wields it |

## 3. Consolidation / equilibrium → FAILs **Plausibility** / **Propagation**

| Gap to find | Why it fails | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| A clean, stable division of power (one domain, one faction) with no force preventing integration | Real power consolidates — mutual dependency drives *buying your supplier*, not stable separation; "it's just stable" is asserted | Name the countervailing force that keeps the cartel from merging — or let it have consolidated |
| One actor *could* dominate (holds identity + money + the record) but doesn't, with no stated reason | "Why hasn't the strongest piece already won?" is unanswered | Give the equilibrium that constrains it (a reputation knife-edge, mutual deterrence, a hard limit) |

## 4. The missing consequence ("what happens when X?") → FAILs **Propagation**

| Gap to find | Why it fails | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| An axiom with an obvious downstream the world hasn't followed (e.g. "tech beats the system + is cheap + persecuted" → why hasn't the underground advanced it?) | A consequence the world's *own logic* should have produced is missing — the system is a snapshot its rules should have already disrupted | Run "And Then What?" until it terminates; either show the consequence or name what suppresses it |
| A capability exists that trivializes a central conflict, unaddressed | The conflict only survives by the author not noticing the exploit | Address the exploit (cost it, bound it, or use it) |

## 5. The unruled god-power (bright-line) → FAILs **Bright-line on escalation**

| Gap to find | Why it fails | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| The system can clearly do a smaller thing, and a *larger, world-breaking* version is one inference away and unruled (heals a body → edits a mind? moves goods → moves anything? unpersons a record → unpersons a person?) | An unruled escalation path is a latent deus ex machina and an unexamined horror | Draw the bright line explicitly: rule the bigger power IN (with cost/limit) or OUT (with reason) |

## 6. The exploit the underground/clever-actor would find → FAILs **Generativity** / **Plausibility**

| Gap to find | Why it fails | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| A motivated, persecuted, intelligent in-world actor has an obvious move the world doesn't show them making | Smart actors don't leave free wins on the table; if they do, the world is being protected by author fiat | Either show them taking it (and follow the consequence) or name why they can't |

---

## Important: stress-tests find gaps, not verdicts

A system that triggers none of these is not automatically sound — the rubric's evidence-anchor cap still applies: to PASS a dimension you must cite the axiom/consequence that *earns* it (the real cost, the real limit, the actual propagation). And every triggered gap gets the **steelman**: if the world has a stated, plausible answer, it's a PASS, not a FAIL. The detectors surface *suspects* for the rubric to rule on.
