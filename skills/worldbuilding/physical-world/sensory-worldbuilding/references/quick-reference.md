# Sensory Worldbuilding — Quick Reference


## Quick Reference

| Location | Signature |
|----------|-----------|
| Desert trading city | Cardamom + camel dung, fabric snapping in wind, sand in everything |
| Underground hold | Echoing hammers, hot metal + mineral water, constant cool |
| Magical academy | Ozone taste, edge-of-hearing hum, skin prickling near spells |
| Coastal village | Salt air, seabird cries, rough rope on hands |
