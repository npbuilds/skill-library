# Wrede Worldbuilding Taxonomy

Patricia Wrede's comprehensive worldbuilding questionnaire — the SFWA standard. 9 domains, 58+ subcategories. Use as a checklist: you don't need to answer every question, but scanning them reveals gaps you didn't know you had.

Source: Science Fiction & Fantasy Writers Association (SFWA)

## 1. The World

### Basics
- Is this an alternate Earth, completely invented world, or Earth with modifications?
- What's the technological/magical level?
- What's the overall tone? (Epic, intimate, dark, hopeful, satirical?)

### Physical Laws
- Do standard physics apply? What's different?
- What about biology — do creatures follow evolutionary principles?
- How does the fantasy system interact with natural law?

## 2. Physical and Historical Features

### Climate & Geography
- How many continents? What shapes are they?
- Major mountain ranges, rivers, deserts, forests?
- Climate patterns — prevailing winds, ocean currents, rain shadows?
- Natural disasters — earthquakes, volcanoes, storms? How often?
- What resources are geographically concentrated?

### Natural Resources
- What's abundant? What's scarce?
- Are there resources unique to this world?
- How does resource distribution create trade and conflict?

### World History
- How old is recorded history?
- What are the major eras?
- What events shaped the current political landscape?
- What's been forgotten or suppressed?

## 3. Magic and Magicians

### Rules of Magic
- What can magic do? What can't it do?
- What are the costs and limits?
- Is it learned, inherited, granted, or random?
- How does it interact with technology?
- Are there different types/schools?

### Practitioners
- How common are magic users?
- How are they trained? (Apprenticeship, academy, self-taught?)
- How are they regarded socially? (Respected, feared, regulated, persecuted?)
- Are there organizations of magic users?
- What's the relationship between magic and political power?

### Magic and Technology
- Does magic replace technology in some areas?
- Are there magical equivalents of technology? (Magical communication, transport, medicine?)
- Do magic and technology conflict?

## 4. Peoples and Customs

### Customs and Social Norms
- How do people greet each other? (By class, gender, age, familiarity?)
- What gestures are polite? Rude? Obscene?
- What are the rules around hospitality?
- Gift-giving customs?
- Attitudes toward privacy, personal space, touching?

### Food and Drink
- Staple foods? Regional specialties?
- Eating customs — who eats with whom? Table manners?
- Sacred or forbidden foods?
- Alcohol/drug use — social attitudes, legal status?

### Language
- How many languages exist?
- Is there a trade language or lingua franca?
- How do languages map to political boundaries?
- Literacy rates — who can read? In which languages?
- Are there sacred or forbidden words?

### Ethics and Values
- What's considered virtuous? What's shameful?
- Attitudes toward lying, theft, killing — context-dependent?
- How are children taught right and wrong?
- Do different cultures have fundamentally different ethical frameworks?

### Religion
- (Cross-reference with religion-design skill)
- How many religions? How do they interact?
- Relationship between religion and politics?
- Attitudes toward atheism, heresy, conversion?

## 5. Social Organization

### Government
- Types of government across different regions?
- How is power transferred? (Hereditary, elected, seized, divine mandate?)
- How much power does the central government have vs. local?
- What checks exist on power? Do they work?
- Bureaucracy — how large, how efficient, how corrupt?

### Politics
- Major political factions? What do they want?
- How does one gain political power? (Birth, wealth, military, magic, popularity?)
- Role of advisors, councils, courts?
- Espionage and intelligence systems?

### Crime and Law
- What's illegal? What's the enforcement mechanism?
- Trial system — juries, judges, combat, divination?
- Punishments — fines, imprisonment, exile, execution, magical?
- Organized crime? Black markets?

### Foreign Relations
- Which nations are allied? Which are rivals?
- Trade agreements? Embargoes?
- Diplomatic conventions — ambassadors, hostages, marriages?
- History of wars — what were they about?

### War
- How are armies organized? Standing army vs. levies?
- Military technology — weapons, armor, fortifications?
- Role of magic in warfare?
- Rules of war — what's forbidden? (And does anyone follow the rules?)
- What happens to prisoners? To civilians?

## 6. Commerce, Trade, and Public Life

### Business and Commerce
- Who controls major industries?
- Guild systems? Merchant classes?
- Banking — loans, interest, letters of credit?
- What's considered an honest living vs. disreputable?

### Transportation
- How do people and goods move? (Roads, rivers, sea, air, magical?)
- Speed of travel between major cities?
- Who maintains roads and infrastructure?
- Dangers of travel — bandits, weather, monsters?

### Science and Technology
- Current state of scientific knowledge?
- Who does research? (Academies, guilds, individual scholars?)
- Attitudes toward new discoveries — welcomed or feared?
- Relationship between science and magic?

### Medicine
- State of medical knowledge?
- Who provides healthcare? (Doctors, healers, priests, magic users?)
- Attitudes toward illness — natural, punishment, magical?
- Life expectancy? Infant mortality? Common causes of death?

### Arts and Entertainment
- Valued art forms? (Music, painting, sculpture, literature, theater?)
- Who are artists — respected craftspeople or marginal figures?
- Entertainment — festivals, sports, games, storytelling?
- Censorship — what can't be depicted or said?

### Architecture
- Building materials available?
- Architectural styles — what do they express about the culture?
- Defensive architecture — walls, moats, magical wards?
- Sacred architecture — temples, shrines, sacred geometry?

## 7. Daily Life

### Fashion and Dress
- What do people wear? How does it vary by class, occupation, region, gender?
- Dress codes — legal or social restrictions?
- What materials are available? What's expensive?
- Symbolic or magical clothing?

### Manners and Social Expectations
- Class system — rigid or fluid?
- Gender roles — prescribed or flexible?
- Age-related expectations — when is one an adult?
- How are outsiders/foreigners treated?

### Diet and Agriculture
- What crops grow in each region?
- Farming methods — how labor-intensive?
- Food preservation — salting, smoking, magical?
- Seasonal eating patterns?

### Education
- Who gets educated? In what?
- Formal schools vs. apprenticeships vs. family teaching?
- What constitutes an educated person?
- Libraries, books, records — who has access?

### Calendar and Time
- How is time measured? (Solar, lunar, magical?)
- How many days in a year? Months? Weeks?
- Named days? Lucky/unlucky days?
- How is age reckoned? When do important transitions happen?

## How to Use This Checklist

1. **Scan all categories** — don't try to answer everything. Just notice which ones you haven't thought about.
2. **Flag gaps that matter** — if your story involves trade, the Commerce section matters. If it doesn't, skim it.
3. **Look for connections** — the most interesting worldbuilding happens where categories interact (religion + government, magic + economics, food + geography).
4. **Revisit after writing** — use this as a diagnostic. If a scene feels thin, check which categories are underdeveloped for that setting.
