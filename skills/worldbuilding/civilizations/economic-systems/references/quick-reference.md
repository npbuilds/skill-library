# Economic Systems — Quick Reference


## Innovative Currency Systems in Fiction

| Work | Currency | Why It Works |
|------|----------|-------------|
| Gladstone's *Craft Sequence* | Soulstuff | Economic transactions become existential |
| Sumner-Smith's *Towers* | Magic as currency | Magical gift = wealth; ungifted = homeless |
| Banks' *Culture* | Post-scarcity | Eliminates economic drama; forces character drama |
| Bear's *Eternal Sky* | Trade route control | Trade is money, money is armies, armies are power |
| Le Guin's *Dispossessed* | No property, labor-based | Economic system IS the thought experiment |
