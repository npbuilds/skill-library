## Do NOT Use — Refuted "Stage-Upfront Ladder"

A commonly circulated stage-by-stage upfront ladder (≈$39M discovery / $44M preclinical / $50M Phase I / $45M Phase II / $300M Phase III) attributed to the JPM Q1 2024 deck was **adversarially refuted (1-of-3 support)** during research — it does not hold up as a benchmark distribution. Do not enshrine a clean phase→upfront ladder; upfronts are dominated by asset-specific competitive dynamics (single bidder vs auction) far more than by phase alone.

**Usage note.** Never quote a single "typical deal" — quote the structure (upfront small, milestones large) and give a stage- and modality-conditioned range. Royalty rate, upfront %, and M&A premium all move with stage at signing, licensor contribution, territory, and number of bidders (single bidder → competitive auction).
