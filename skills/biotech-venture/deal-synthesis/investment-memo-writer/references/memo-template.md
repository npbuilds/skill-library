## Writing Conventions

| Rule | Example |
|---|---|
| **Lead with assertion** | "PoS is 18%, 2x the oncology base rate" NOT "We calculated PoS to be..." |
| **Quantify every claim** | "$2.3B peak sales" NOT "large commercial opportunity" |
| **State assumptions explicitly** | "Assuming 15% market share at peak (3rd entrant)" |
| **Use confidence language** | "Confirmed: target validated by MR" / "Speculative: efficacy in combination" |
| **Flag what you don't know** | "Unknown: duration of response beyond 12 months" |
| **Keep it concise** | Total memo: 3-5 pages. IC partners read the exec summary; analysts read the detail. |
