# Diligence Scorecard — 8-Pillar Scoring Calibration

## Scoring Scale (1-10 per pillar)

| Score | Label | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| 9-10 | Exceptional | Top 5% of assets evaluated; clear competitive advantage |
| 7-8 | Strong | Above average; meaningful strengths with manageable risks |
| 5-6 | Adequate | Average for stage; no clear differentiation but no dealbreakers |
| 3-4 | Weak | Below average; significant concerns that require mitigation |
| 1-2 | Critical | Fundamental problems; likely disqualifying unless addressed |

## Pillar 1: Clinical Strength (Weight: 20%)

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 9-10 | Positive Phase 2/3 data exceeding expectations; validated surrogate endpoint; biomarker-selected population; PoS >25% from current phase |
| 7-8 | Positive Phase 2 data at/near expectations; reasonable endpoint strategy; PoS 15-25% |
| 5-6 | Phase 1 safety data with encouraging signals; standard endpoint; PoS 8-15% |
| 3-4 | Preclinical only; unvalidated endpoint; high-risk population; PoS 4-8% |
| 1-2 | No clinical data; novel/untested endpoint; PoS <4% |

## Pillar 2: Regulatory Positioning (Weight: 15%)

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 9-10 | BTD granted; clear FDA precedent for endpoint/indication; orphan designation; strong type A/B meeting feedback |
| 7-8 | Fast Track granted; FDA-accepted endpoint; some regulatory precedent |
| 5-6 | Standard pathway; validated endpoint class but no specific FDA feedback |
| 3-4 | Novel endpoint without precedent; potential regulatory hurdles; no FDA interaction |
| 1-2 | Endpoint FDA has previously rejected; known regulatory resistance; CRL risk factors present |

## Pillar 3: Competitive Position (Weight: 15%)

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 9-10 | First-in-class with validated mechanism; clear differentiation on efficacy + safety + convenience; 2+ year lead over nearest competitor |
| 7-8 | Best-in-class potential; clear differentiation on 1-2 axes; 1-2 year lead |
| 5-6 | Me-better with modest differentiation; comparable timeline to competitors |
| 3-4 | Me-too with limited differentiation; behind competitors; crowded indication |
| 1-2 | No differentiation; 4th+ entrant in saturated class; standard of care shifting away |

## Pillar 4: Manufacturing Feasibility (Weight: 10%)

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 9-10 | Well-established manufacturing process; COGS <15% net sales; no supply chain risks; multiple CMOs available |
| 7-8 | Standard modality with known manufacturing path; COGS 15-25%; manageable scale-up |
| 5-6 | Moderate complexity; COGS 25-35%; some scale-up uncertainty |
| 3-4 | Complex modality (gene/cell therapy); COGS >35%; limited CMO options; potency assay challenges |
| 1-2 | Novel manufacturing with no precedent; COGS >50%; single-source supply chain; quality concerns |

## Pillar 5: IP Fortress (Weight: 10%)

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 9-10 | Composition-of-matter patent; 12+ years effective life; no FTO issues; multiple exclusivity layers (orphan + NCE + patent) |
| 7-8 | Strong patent portfolio; 8-12 years life; clean FTO; single exclusivity type |
| 5-6 | Formulation/method patents; 5-8 years life; minor FTO concerns |
| 3-4 | Weak patent coverage; <5 years remaining; active FTO challenges |
| 1-2 | No composition patent; imminent LOE; active litigation; biosimilar competition |

## Pillar 6: Financial Attractiveness (Weight: 15%)

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 9-10 | rNPV > $500M; peak sales > $3B; IRR > 30%; multiple value inflection points ahead |
| 7-8 | rNPV $200-500M; peak sales $1-3B; IRR 20-30% |
| 5-6 | rNPV $50-200M; peak sales $500M-1B; IRR 15-20% |
| 3-4 | rNPV $0-50M; peak sales $200-500M; IRR 10-15% |
| 1-2 | Negative rNPV; peak sales <$200M; IRR <10% |

## Pillar 7: Team & Execution (Weight: 10%)

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 9-10 | Management with prior FDA approvals; deep TA expertise; strong board (VC/industry); >3yr runway |
| 7-8 | Experienced team with relevant backgrounds; adequate funding through next catalyst |
| 5-6 | Mixed team; some relevant experience; funding adequate but tight |
| 3-4 | First-time team; limited TA expertise; <1yr runway without raise |
| 1-2 | Management turnover; no relevant experience; at-risk financing |

## Pillar 8: Computational Infrastructure (Weight: 5%) — NEW

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 9-10 | Proprietary AI/ML platform with demonstrated acceleration of timelines; large proprietary dataset; integrated wet-lab/dry-lab; computational team on founding team |
| 7-8 | Active AI/ML integration in discovery/development; meaningful data assets; computational capability |
| 5-6 | Standard computational tools; no proprietary platform; external partnerships for AI/ML |
| 3-4 | Limited computational capability; no data differentiation; traditional approach only |
| 1-2 | No computational infrastructure; no data strategy |

## Aggregate Scoring

```
Aggregate Score = Σ (Pillar Score × Pillar Weight)

Recommendation Bands:
  8.0-10.0: Strong Buy — exceptional opportunity, allocate maximum position
  6.5-7.9:  Buy — attractive risk/reward, standard position
  5.0-6.4:  Hold — fair value, monitor for catalysts
  3.5-4.9:  Cautious — below average, only with specific thesis
  1.0-3.4:  Avoid — fundamental concerns, do not invest
```

## Red Flags (Override Scoring)

Any of these automatically caps the aggregate at 4.0 regardless of pillar scores:

| Red Flag | Description |
|---|---|
| Going concern risk | <6 months cash, no clear financing path |
| Integrity concern | Data manipulation, SEC investigation, restatements |
| Regulatory rejection | CRL received with no clear path to resubmission |
| Patent invalidation | Core composition patent invalidated or expired |
| Management exodus | CEO + CSO departure within 6 months |

## Green Flags (Score Boosters)

These add +0.5 to the aggregate (cap at 10.0):

| Green Flag | Description |
|---|---|
| Insider buying | C-suite open-market purchases >$500K in last 6 months |
| Institutional validation | Top-tier biotech VC led recent round (Arch, Flagship, Third Rock, ARCH) |
| Strategic partnership | Big pharma co-development deal with opt-in |
| Platform expansion | Multiple indications advancing from single platform |
