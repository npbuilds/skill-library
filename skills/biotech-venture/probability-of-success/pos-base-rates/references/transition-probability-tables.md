# Transition Probability Tables — Extended Reference

## Oncology Sub-Indications

Success rates vary dramatically within oncology. Using a single "oncology" rate masks important variation.

| Sub-Indication | P1->P2 | P2->P3 | P3->NDA | LOA (P1) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Solid tumor (all) | 42% | 22% | 50% | 3.7% | Heterogeneous, high competition |
| NSCLC | 40% | 20% | 48% | 3.1% | Crowded, IO-dominated |
| Breast cancer | 45% | 25% | 55% | 5.0% | Better-defined subtypes |
| Hematologic malignancy | 55% | 30% | 60% | 8.3% | More homogeneous, better endpoints |
| AML/MDS | 48% | 25% | 52% | 5.0% | High unmet need, genetic stratification |
| Multiple myeloma | 58% | 35% | 62% | 10.5% | Multiple approved combos, clear endpoints |
| Rare/pediatric tumors | 60% | 38% | 60% | 11.4% | Orphan incentives, smaller trials |

## LOA by Current Phase (All Indications)

When evaluating an asset, you need the LOA from its current phase, not just Phase 1.

| Current Phase | LOA to Approval | Key Inflection |
|---|---|---|
| Preclinical | ~5% (est.) | IND filing is first gate |
| Phase 1 | 7.9% | Safety/tolerability |
| Phase 2 | 15.2% | Efficacy signal — the "valley of death" gate |
| Phase 3 | 52.4% | Confirmatory — most expensive phase |
| NDA/BLA filed | 88% | Regulatory review |
| Advisory Committee | 92% | Post-AdComm to approval |

## Value Inflection by Phase

The change in LOA at each phase transition creates discrete value inflections — this is what drives biotech investing returns.

| Transition | LOA Before | LOA After | LOA Multiple | rNPV Impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Phase 1 -> Phase 2 (positive) | 7.9% | 15.2% | 1.9x | ~2x valuation |
| Phase 2 -> Phase 3 (positive) | 15.2% | 52.4% | 3.4x | ~3-4x valuation |
| Phase 3 -> NDA filed | 52.4% | 88% | 1.7x | ~1.5-2x valuation |
| NDA -> Approval | 88% | 100% | 1.1x | ~1.1x + commercial ramp |

The Phase 2 -> Phase 3 transition is the largest value inflection in drug development. This is why biotech VCs disproportionately target Phase 2 assets.

## Source Vintage and Limitations

| Source | Period | Sample Size | Key Limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| BIO/Informa/QLS | 2011-2020 | 15,000+ programs | Pre-dates some modality shifts (mRNA, bispecifics) |
| BioMedTracker/Pharmapremia | 2014-2023 | Updated annually | Proprietary; rates declining vs BIO data |
| Wong et al. (Biostatistics) | 2000-2015 | 21,143 programs | Older vintage; well-cited academic source |
| Hay et al. (Nature Biotech) | 2003-2011 | 4,451 programs | Foundational paper but dated |

When using these tables:
- Apply the most recent available data for the therapeutic area
- Note the vintage year in any PoS calculation
- Be aware that all base rates are retrospective — they describe what happened, not what will happen
- Specific program characteristics (mechanism, biomarker, team) can move PoS significantly above or below base rates
