# Relationship Stewardship — Quick Reference


## Quick Reference

| Tier | Definition | Cadence | Touchpoint Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Inner Circle** (5–15 people) | Mentors, deep collaborators, close advisors, people whose career you'd actively support | Quarterly minimum | Substantive — phone, in-person, deep emails |
| **Active Network** (50–150 people) | Colleagues, peer professionals, useful operators in your domain | Twice-yearly minimum | Moderate — emails, intros, conference catch-ups |
| **Latent Network** (500+ people) | Acquaintances, conference connections, weak ties | Annually or signal-driven | Light — LinkedIn engagement, the occasional ping |

## Quick Reference

| Mode | Mechanism | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Calendar-driven** | "Email X every quarter" | Regular, won't drop them | Reads as transactional; touchpoints lack reason |
| **Signal-driven** | Touchpoint when they post / publish / move / win | Reads as engaged; touchpoint is meaningful | Can drop people who go quiet |
