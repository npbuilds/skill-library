# Mentorship Design — Quick Reference


## Sponsor vs Mentor vs Advisor vs Coach

| Role | What They Do | Cadence | Compensation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mentor** | Counsel, share patterns, occasional intros | Quarterly to monthly | Free |
| **Sponsor** | Active advocacy, opens doors, takes risk on you | Embedded in current org | Free (you owe results) |
| **Advisor** | Specific domain advice; sometimes equity-compensated | Monthly | Often equity (early-stage) |
| **Coach** | Structured personal development; trained role | Weekly to monthly | Paid ($300–$1000+/hour) |

## Quick Reference

| Cadence | Format | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| Quarterly 60-min | Phone or in-person | Most senior mentorships |
| Monthly 30-min | Phone | High-touch relationships in transition periods |
| Annual deep-dive | Half-day or longer | Strategic / long-term mentorships |
| Ad-hoc on triggers | Email or short call | Mature relationships where you've built trust |
