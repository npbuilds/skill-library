# Executive Interview Prep — Quick Reference


## The Canonical Executive Interview Loop

| Round | What's Tested | Typical Format |
|---|---|---|
| **CEO conversation** | Trust, fit, strategic chemistry | 60–90 min |
| **Board / chair conversation** | Governance literacy, board-management | 45–60 min |
| **Existing team panel** | Team-fit, collaboration patterns | 60 min |
| **Strategic / scenario** | "How would you handle X?" | 60–90 min |
| **100-day plan presentation** | Sometimes formal, sometimes informal | 60–90 min |
| **Reference round** | What does the network say | Backchannel |

## Common Failure Modes

| Failure | Looks Like | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Over-clinical | Clinical solutions to operating problems | Surface the operating tradeoffs, not just the science |
| No 100-day plan | "I'd want to see what's there before I plan" | Have a structured plan even if you'll modify on contact |
| Wishful operator | "I'd build a great team" | Be specific — who, when, what gaps |
| Board-naive | Doesn't understand board cadence or dynamics | Demonstrate you've thought about board management |
| No P&L sense | Avoids questions about cost/capital | Have a view, even if the numbers will be wrong |
