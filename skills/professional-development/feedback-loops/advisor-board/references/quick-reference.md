# Advisor Board — Quick Reference


## Quick Reference

| Perspective | What They Provide |
|---|---|
| **Domain-deep senior** | Pattern recognition in your specific field (clinical development, biotech) |
| **Adjacent senior** | Different lens (operator, investor, scientist, lawyer) |
| **One-step-ahead** | Someone who's just made the move you're considering |
| **Cross-industry / outsider** | Different vocabulary; surfaces what insiders miss |
| **Peer / accountability** | Someone at your level who can be brutally honest |
| **Cultural / values** | Someone who knows you well enough to flag values drift |
| **Network connector** | Hub who can introduce you across the ecosystem |

## Designing the Cadence

| Cadence | Format | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| **Quarterly 45–60 min** | Phone, video, in-person | Most advisors |
| **Monthly during high-intensity periods** | Phone | Pre-transition, during transition |
| **Annual deep-dive** | Half-day, in-person | Strategic-level advisors |
| **Ad-hoc on triggers** | Email or short call | Mature relationships |

## Common Failure Modes

| Failure | Looks Like | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Diffuse "mentor network" | Many vague relationships | Pick 4–7 explicit advisors with named perspectives |
| Too senior | Only "people 3 levels above me" | Include peers and cross-perspective advisors |
| No cadence | Reach out only when in crisis | Quarterly cadence at minimum |
| No agenda | Drift conversations | 24h-advance agenda |
| Asking for permission | "Should I take this?" | Bring the analysis; ask for sharpening |
| No reciprocity | Take-take dynamic | Active reciprocity |
| Permanence | Same advisors for 10 years through life changes | Annual composition refresh |
