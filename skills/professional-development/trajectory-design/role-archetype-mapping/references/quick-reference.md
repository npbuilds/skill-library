# Role Archetype Mapping — Quick Reference


## The Biotech-Adjacent Career Archetypes (For an MD-Operator Trajectory)

| Archetype | Day-to-Day Work | Credential Path | Comp Range | Optionality |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Clinical Operator (Director, VP, CMO)** | Trial design, regulatory interaction, team leadership, board updates | Continued operating; CMO often requires SVP or Head-of step first | $250K–$1M+ all-in | High → CMO/founder/board paths |
| **Biotech Founder (Scientific or Operating)** | Pitch decks, hiring, board management, fundraising, every fire | Often a clinical or scientific specialty + the choice to start | $0–$300K base + significant equity | Highest variance |
| **Biotech VC Associate** | Deal screening, diligence support, portfolio updates, sourcing | Often analyst-track from consulting/finance/banking; sometimes operator-pivot | $150K–$300K + carry | Toward principal/partner over 5–10 years |
| **Biotech VC Principal/Partner** | IC participation, deal leadership, board service, sourcing | Usually post-associate or post-strong-operator track | $300K–$1M+ + carry | Toward general partner or back to operating |
| **Venture Partner** | Part-time advisor + occasional deal lead; often retains operator role | Senior operator credibility + warm relationship with firm | Modest cash + carry on deals you lead | Hybrid; preserves operator optionality |
| **EIR (Entrepreneur in Residence)** | Exploring startups inside a firm, often building one | Strong operator credibility + firm relationship | $200K–$400K base + future equity | Toward founder or operating role |
| **Operating Partner** | Advising portfolio companies; sometimes deal sourcing | Senior operator with broad network | $200K–$500K + carry on advised deals | Toward partner role or back to operating |
| **Independent Advisor** | Multiple part-time advisor roles; often equity-compensated | Senior operator credibility + reputation | Mostly equity; modest cash | Maximum flexibility |
| **Board Director** | 6–10 board meetings/year per board; governance, CEO support | Senior operator credibility + board readiness | $30K–$200K cash + equity per board | Compounds with multiple seats |

## Quick Reference

| Dimension | What It Means | Typical Spectrum |
|---|---|---|
| **Capital deployment vs operations** | Are you allocating capital or executing programs | Operator → Operating Partner → VC Partner |
| **Time horizon of feedback** | How long until you know if your decisions worked | Short (operator quarterly) → Long (VC 5–10 years) |
| **Cash vs equity weighting** | Where the upside lives | Operator (cash-heavy) → Founder (equity-heavy) |
| **Visibility / reputation** | How public the role is | Variable; founder/partner often most public |
| **Optionality preserved** | What doors stay open | Generalist roles preserve more; specialist roles compound |
| **Day-to-day energy demand** | Continuous high-intensity vs episodic | Founder (continuous) vs board director (episodic) |

## Common Failure Modes

| Failure | Looks Like | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Title-driven, not work-driven | "I want to be a VC partner" without knowing what partners actually do | Day-in-the-life interviews |
| Over-romanticized founder | Underestimating the operational reality | Talk to founders who failed, not just those who succeeded |
| Underestimating ramp-up | Expecting to be effective in 3 months in a new archetype | Plan for 12–24 month ramp-up to senior effectiveness |
| Ignoring energy fit | Targeting an archetype because it's prestigious, not because it suits you | The honest energy audit |
| Misreading optionality | Assuming a role preserves more options than it does | Check by examining what people in that role do *next* |
