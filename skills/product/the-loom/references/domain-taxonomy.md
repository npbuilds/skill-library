# Domain Taxonomy — The Product Skill Map

## Architecture Overview

```
THE LOOM (orchestrator) — AI-Native Product Orchestrator
│
├── ① SENSE (director) — "What's becoming possible?"
│   ├── frontier-antenna (action)      Translate AI developments into product opportunities
│   ├── emergence-detector (action)    Notice unexpected capability combinations
│   ├── signal-reader (knowledge)      Frameworks for reading weak signals
│   └── capability-radar (action)      Living map of possible vs. surfaced capabilities
│
├── ② ENVISION (director) — "What should this intelligence become?"
│   ├── vision-architect (action)      Design system future state in experiential terms
│   ├── paradigm-designer (knowledge)  Catalog and create AI interaction paradigms
│   ├── possibility-mapper (action)    Map full possibility space before filtering
│   └── thesis-forge (action)          Crystallize falsifiable product theses
│
├── ③ SEED (director) — "How do we plant conditions for emergence?"
│   ├── condition-designer (action)    Design initial conditions for product emergence
│   ├── constraint-sculptor (knowledge) Frameworks for constraints as creative tools
│   ├── prototype-grower (action)      Rapid capability prototyping (living demos)
│   └── feedback-architect (action)    Design feedback loops that shape evolution
│
├── ④ SURFACE (director) — "How does the intelligence meet the world?"
│   ├── interface-philosopher (knowledge) Epistemology of AI interfaces
│   ├── exposure-strategist (action)    Decide what to surface, when, to whom
│   ├── experience-weaver (action)      Design emotional/aesthetic quality of interaction
│   └── value-architect (action)        Design value exchange for intelligence products
│
├── ⑤ EVOLVE (director) — "How does the system grow from use?"
│   ├── adaptation-observer (action)    Watch product-in-use vs. product-as-seeded
│   ├── amplifier (action)              Strengthen emergent successful patterns
│   ├── pruning-engine (action)         Decide what to compost, extract learnings
│   └── learning-loops (knowledge)      Frameworks for self-improving systems
│
└── ⑥ SYNTHESIZE (director) — "What's the meta-pattern across everything?"
    ├── pattern-weaver (action)         Cross-product pattern recognition
    ├── initiative-tracker (action)     Organic lifecycle tracking
    ├── product-briefing-engine (action) Weekly Product Synthesis
    └── narrative-keeper (action)       Maintain living product story
```

## Cross-Domain Dependencies

### Sense Director
- `frontier-antenna` depends on: neocortex/frontier-scanner (consumes frontier scans)
- `emergence-detector` touches: all domain orchestrators (monitors cross-domain interactions)
- `signal-reader` depends on: spelunker (research), archon (market timing intelligence)
- `capability-radar` depends on: infrastructure-orchestrator (registry health), neocortex/architecture (gap maps)

### Envision Director
- `vision-architect` engages: design-orchestrator (aesthetic identity), prose-orchestrator (narrative)
- `paradigm-designer` engages: design-orchestrator, philosophy-orchestrator (epistemology)
- `possibility-mapper` engages: game-theory-orchestrator (option value), neocortex/scenario-planner
- `thesis-forge` engages: spelunker (validation research)

### Seed Director
- `condition-designer` engages: worldbuilding-orchestrator (systems coherence)
- `constraint-sculptor` engages: game-theory/mechanism-design (incentive boundaries), philosophy (ethics)
- `prototype-grower` engages: master-artificer (creative coding), domain orchestrators as needed
- `feedback-architect` engages: data-science-orchestrator (metrics), game-theory (incentive design)

### Surface Director
- `interface-philosopher` engages: philosophy-orchestrator, design-orchestrator
- `exposure-strategist` engages: game-theory (progressive disclosure), prose-orchestrator (narrative pacing)
- `experience-weaver` engages: design-orchestrator (creative brief), prose-orchestrator (voice/tone)
- `value-architect` engages: game-theory/mechanism-design, archon (market dynamics), prose-orchestrator (value framing)

### Evolve Director
- `adaptation-observer` engages: data-science-orchestrator (behavioral analysis)
- `amplifier` engages: neocortex/growth-architect (build prioritization)
- `pruning-engine` engages: infrastructure-orchestrator (cleanup)
- `learning-loops` engages: data-science (modeling), game-theory (learning in games)

### Synthesize Director
- `pattern-weaver` touches: all orchestrators (cross-product synthesis)
- `initiative-tracker` is self-contained (reads/writes loom-briefings/)
- `product-briefing-engine` consumes: all director outputs
- `narrative-keeper` engages: prose-orchestrator (narrative craft)

## Curriculum Ordering

Within each director, skills have a natural learning sequence:

### Sense
```
signal-reader (frameworks first) → frontier-antenna (apply to frontier) → capability-radar (map the landscape) → emergence-detector (watch for surprises)
```

### Envision
```
paradigm-designer (know the paradigms) → possibility-mapper (explore the space) → vision-architect (choose a direction) → thesis-forge (crystallize)
```

### Seed
```
constraint-sculptor (understand constraints) → condition-designer (design conditions) → feedback-architect (design loops) → prototype-grower (grow it)
```

### Surface
```
interface-philosopher (philosophy first) → exposure-strategist (what to expose) → experience-weaver (how it feels) → value-architect (how value flows)
```

### Evolve
```
adaptation-observer (observe first) → learning-loops (understand learning) → amplifier (strengthen good) → pruning-engine (remove bad)
```

### Synthesize
```
initiative-tracker (know the state) → pattern-weaver (see patterns) → product-briefing-engine (synthesize) → narrative-keeper (tell the story)
```
