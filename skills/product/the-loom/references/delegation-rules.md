# Delegation Rules — Director Routing Logic

## Single-Director Routing

When the question clearly maps to one cognitive mode, route directly.

**Signal → Director mapping:**

| User Signal | Route To | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "What's new?", "what changed in AI?", "any new capabilities?" | Sense | Frontier awareness through product lens |
| "What's becoming possible?", "what can we build now that we couldn't before?" | Sense | Capability radar activation |
| "I noticed something unexpected", "this skill does something I didn't plan" | Sense (emergence-detector) | Emergence signal |
| "What's the market doing?", "timing", "is it too early?", "adoption curves" | Sense (signal-reader) | External signal reading |
| "What should this become?", "what's the vision?", "long-term direction" | Envision | Vision architecture |
| "What kind of product is this?", "what paradigm?", "conversational or visual?" | Envision (paradigm-designer) | Interaction paradigm selection |
| "What could we build?", "show me the options", "possibility space" | Envision (possibility-mapper) | Possibility mapping |
| "What's the thesis?", "why this?", "what do we believe?" | Envision (thesis-forge) | Thesis crystallization |
| "How do we start?", "what are the initial conditions?", "seed this" | Seed | Condition design |
| "Too much scope", "constrain this", "what are the boundaries?" | Seed (constraint-sculptor) | Constraint sculpting |
| "Show me a prototype", "what would this look like alive?", "demo" | Seed (prototype-grower) | Rapid capability prototyping |
| "What signals should we watch?", "how will we know it's working?" | Seed (feedback-architect) | Feedback loop design |
| "How should users interact with this?", "what's the right interface?" | Surface | Interface philosophy |
| "What do we expose first?", "sequencing", "what stays hidden?" | Surface (exposure-strategist) | Exposure strategy |
| "What should it feel like?", "the experience", "emotional quality" | Surface (experience-weaver) | Experience design |
| "How do we price this?", "value model", "free or paid?", "monetization" | Surface (value-architect) | Value exchange design |
| "How is it changing?", "what are users doing?", "unexpected usage" | Evolve | Adaptation observation |
| "This is working — how do we get more of it?" | Evolve (amplifier) | Pattern amplification |
| "This isn't working", "should we cut this?", "simplify" | Evolve (pruning-engine) | Pruning decisions |
| "How does it get better on its own?", "self-improvement", "learning" | Evolve (learning-loops) | Self-improvement design |
| "What's the big picture?", "across all products", "meta-pattern" | Synthesize | Pattern weaving |
| "Where are all initiatives?", "status", "what's in flight?" | Synthesize (initiative-tracker) | Initiative lifecycle |
| "Brief me", "what should I know this week?", "product synthesis" | Synthesize (product-briefing-engine) | Weekly briefing |
| "What's the story?", "narrative", "how has our thinking evolved?" | Synthesize (narrative-keeper) | Product narrative |

## Multi-Director Routing

Most real product questions span multiple cognitive modes. Analyze in dependency order.

### The Loom Cycle (Full Cultivation)

For comprehensive product cultivation, the full cycle runs:

**① Sense → ② Envision → ③ Seed → ④ Surface → ⑤ Evolve → ⑥ Synthesize**

Each phase receives the output of prior phases as context. But the cycle is NOT always linear — you can enter at any phase.

### Common Multi-Director Sequences

**"I have a new product idea"**
→ Sense (capability-radar: can we actually build this?) → Envision (thesis-forge: crystallize the thesis) → Seed (condition-designer: what are the initial conditions?)

**"A product is live but not growing"**
→ Evolve (adaptation-observer: what's actually happening?) → Sense (emergence-detector: any unexpected patterns?) → Seed (feedback-architect: are the loops right?)

**"I want to expose something new"**
→ Sense (capability-radar: is it ready?) → Surface (exposure-strategist: how and when?) → Surface (experience-weaver: what should it feel like?) → Surface (value-architect: what's the exchange?)

**"Everything feels stuck"**
→ Synthesize (product-briefing-engine: zoom out) → Evolve (pruning-engine: what to cut?) → Envision (possibility-mapper: what else could we build?)

**"I want to understand the portfolio"**
→ Synthesize (initiative-tracker: current state) → Synthesize (pattern-weaver: cross-product patterns) → Sense (capability-radar: untapped capability) → Envision (possibility-mapper: white space)

**"A frontier shift just happened"**
→ Sense (frontier-antenna: what does this enable?) → Envision (possibility-mapper: new possibility space) → Envision (thesis-forge: new theses) → Seed (condition-designer: rapid prototype of highest-potential thesis)

**"Should we kill this initiative?"**
→ Evolve (adaptation-observer: what's the signal?) → Evolve (pruning-engine: compost analysis — what learnings to extract) → Synthesize (narrative-keeper: update the product story) → Sense (capability-radar: what does the freed capability enable?)

### Cross-Domain Engagement Patterns

When a director needs domain expertise, The Loom brokers the engagement:

| Director Need | Domain Orchestrator | What The Loom Provides as Context |
|---|---|---|
| Sense needs frontier intelligence | neocortex/frontier-scanner | "Translate these AI developments into product opportunities" |
| Envision needs vision articulation | prose-orchestrator | Product thesis, target experience, voice requirements |
| Envision needs paradigm exploration | design-orchestrator, philosophy-orchestrator | "What interaction paradigms fit this intelligence type?" |
| Seed needs systems coherence | worldbuilding-orchestrator | System rules, constraint boundaries, behavioral expectations |
| Seed needs rapid prototype | master-artificer | Capability combination spec, desired demonstration |
| Surface needs experience design | design-orchestrator | Experience brief with emotional/aesthetic requirements |
| Surface needs positioning language | prose-orchestrator | Positioning thesis, audience, competitive context |
| Surface needs pricing logic | game-theory/mechanism-design | Player model, value flows, incentive structure |
| Surface needs market context | archon | Market landscape, timing signals, comparable products |
| Evolve needs behavioral analysis | data-science-orchestrator | Usage patterns, behavioral data, metric definitions |
| Evolve needs incentive diagnosis | game-theory-orchestrator | "Why are users behaving this way? What incentive is driving it?" |
| Evolve needs deep investigation | spelunker | Specific research question with required evidence quality |

## Conflict Resolution

When directors disagree:

| Conflict | Resolution |
|---|---|
| Sense says "possible" but Envision says "not aligned with vision" | **Envision wins.** Capability without vision is distraction. |
| Envision says "build this" but Seed says "conditions aren't right" | **Seed wins.** Premature cultivation produces weak growth. |
| Seed says "needs more time" but Surface says "users are asking" | **Evaluate the signal strength.** Strong demand can override slow germination — surface a limited exposure. |
| Surface says "expose this" but Evolve says "it's degrading" | **Evolve wins.** Never expose something that's getting worse. Fix the loop first. |
| Multiple directors are engaged but the timeline is tight | **Synthesize arbitrates.** Pattern-weaver checks which combination has worked before. |

## When NOT to Use The Loom

Route directly to a domain orchestrator when:

- The question is purely within one domain ("make this logo better" → design-orchestrator)
- The question is about library health, not product health ("what skills are stale?" → neocortex)
- The question is a research task, not a product task ("is X true?" → spelunker)
- The question is about infrastructure ("fix the registry scanner" → infrastructure-orchestrator)

**The Loom adds value through synthesis.** If there's nothing to synthesize, don't invoke it.
