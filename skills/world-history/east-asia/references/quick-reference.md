# East Asia — Quick Reference


## Regional Timeline

| Period | China | Japan | Korea | Steppe |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Classical** (1000 BCE-220 CE) | Zhou, Warring States, Qin, Han | Jomon/Yayoi | Proto-kingdoms | Xiongnu confederation |
| **Post-Han** (220-589) | Three Kingdoms, fragmentation | Kofun period | Three Kingdoms of Korea | Turkic empires |
| **Tang-Song** (618-1279) | Tang cosmopolitanism, Song economic revolution | Heian court culture | Unified Silla, Goryeo | Khitan, Jurchen |
| **Mongol** (1206-1368) | Yuan dynasty (Mongol rule) | Kamakura (repelled Mongols) | Goryeo under Mongol influence | Mongol Empire |
| **Ming-Qing** (1368-1912) | Ming restoration, Qing expansion | Muromachi, Tokugawa isolation | Joseon dynasty | Manchu/Qing |
| **Modern** (1839-present) | Opium Wars, revolution, Mao, Deng | Meiji, imperialism, postwar miracle | Colonial, division, industrialization | Soviet/post-Soviet Central Asia |
