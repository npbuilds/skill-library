# Demographic And Structural Forces — Quick Reference


## Quick Reference

| Stage | Birth Rate | Death Rate | Population | Historical Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Pre-transition | High | High | Stable/slow growth | All societies before 1750 |
| 2. Early transition | High | Falling | Rapid growth | Victorian Britain, modern sub-Saharan Africa |
| 3. Late transition | Falling | Low | Growth slowing | East Asia 1960s-2000s |
| 4. Post-transition | Low | Low | Stable/declining | Japan, Europe, South Korea today |

## The Secular Cycle

```
EXPANSION PHASE (~100 years):
  Population grows → labor abundant → wages fall → land values rise
  → elites prosper → state revenue grows → stability

STAGFLATION PHASE (~50-100 years):
  Population pressure → Malthusian ceiling approached
  → Wages stagnate → Inequality rises → Elite overproduction begins
  → Fiscal strain → Social tensions increase

CRISIS PHASE (~50 years):
  Elite fragmentation → Fiscal bankruptcy → Popular unrest
  → Civil war / revolution / state collapse → Population decline (war, plague, famine)

DEPRESSION / RECOVERY (~50 years):
  Population low → Labor scarce → Wages rise → Inequality falls
  → New elite cohesion → State rebuilds → → Next expansion
```
