# Americas And Oceania — Quick Reference


## Regional Timeline

| Period | Americas | Pacific/Oceania |
|---|---|---|
| **Pre-contact** (15,000 BCE-1492) | Migration from Asia; development of agriculture (maize, potatoes); Maya, Aztec, Inca, Mississippian, Pueblo, Amazonian civilizations | Austronesian expansion (~3000 BCE); settlement of Polynesia (500 BCE-1200 CE); Polynesian navigation to Hawaii, New Zealand, Easter Island |
| **Contact and conquest** (1492-1600) | Columbus; Cortes and the Aztecs (1519-21); Pizarro and the Inca (1532); demographic collapse (50-90% from disease) | Magellan (1521); Spanish Philippines; limited initial contact |
| **Colonial** (1600-1800) | Spanish viceroyalties; Portuguese Brazil; British/French North America; plantation slavery; mission system | Dutch East Indies; British Australia (1788); increasing European contact |
| **Independence** (1776-1830s) | American Revolution (1776); Haitian Revolution (1791-1804); Latin American independence (Bolivar, San Martin, 1810s-20s) | Continued colonization; Maori resistance |
| **Nation-building** (1830s-present) | US expansion; Mexican-American War; US Civil War; Latin American caudillismo; 20th c. revolutions (Mexico, Cuba); Cold War interventions | Australian/NZ independence; Pacific WWII theater; decolonization; nuclear testing; climate vulnerability |

## Pre-Columbian Civilizations

| Civilization | Region | Peak | Key Achievements |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Maya** | Mesoamerica | 250-900 CE | Writing system; base-20 mathematics with zero; astronomical precision; monumental architecture |
| **Aztec (Mexica)** | Central Mexico | 1325-1521 | Tenochtitlan (~200,000 people, larger than most European cities); tributary empire; chinampas (floating gardens) |
| **Inca** | Andes | 1438-1533 | Largest pre-Columbian empire; 25,000+ miles of roads; quipu record-keeping; vertical archipelago agriculture |
| **Mississippian** | North America | 800-1600 | Cahokia (~20,000 people, largest city in North America before Philadelphia); mound building; trade networks |
| **Polynesian** | Pacific | 500 BCE-1200 CE | Navigated thousands of miles of open ocean using stars, waves, and winds; settled the most dispersed civilization on Earth |
