# Social Movements And Identity — Quick Reference


## Major Movements Across History

| Movement | Period | Key Achievement | Unfinished Business |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Abolitionism** | 1780s-1888 | Ended legal slavery in the Americas and Europe | Racial inequality persisted through Jim Crow, apartheid, structural racism |
| **Suffrage** | 1840s-1920s (1st wave) | Women's right to vote in most democracies | Formal political equality did not produce economic or social equality |
| **Labor** | 1840s-1970s (peak) | 8-hour day, minimum wage, workplace safety, collective bargaining | Deunionization since 1980s; gig economy; global labor arbitrage |
| **Anti-colonial nationalism** | 1920s-1975 | Independence for ~80 new states | Neo-colonialism, economic dependency, post-colonial state failure |
| **US Civil Rights** | 1954-1968 (peak) | Desegregation, Voting Rights Act, Civil Rights Act | Mass incarceration, wealth gap, educational segregation persist |
| **Feminism (2nd wave)** | 1960s-1980s | Reproductive rights, workplace anti-discrimination, Title IX | Pay gap, underrepresentation, violence against women persist |
| **LGBTQ rights** | 1969-present | Decriminalization, marriage equality, anti-discrimination protections | Global variation; criminalized in ~70 countries |
| **Environmentalism** | 1960s-present | EPA, Clean Air/Water Acts, Paris Agreement | Climate change accelerating; institutional responses insufficient |
