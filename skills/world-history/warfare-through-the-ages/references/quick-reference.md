# Warfare Through The Ages — Quick Reference


## Quick Reference

| RMA | Technology | Organizational Change | Societal Impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chariot | Spoke wheel, horse domestication | Palace armies, specialized warriors | Centralized Bronze Age states |
| Infantry | Iron weapons, shield formations | Citizen armies | Greek democracy, Roman republicanism |
| Gunpowder | Cannon, firearms | Standing armies, drill, fortification | Centralized nation-states |
| Industrial | Railroad, telegraph, rifled firearms | Mass mobilization, general staffs | Total war, mass casualties |
| Nuclear | Atomic weapons, ICBMs | Deterrence postures, arms control | Cold War stability (and terror) |
| Information | Precision weapons, cyber, networks | Network-centric warfare, special ops | Blurred state/non-state boundaries |
