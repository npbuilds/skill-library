# Sub Saharan Africa — Quick Reference


## Regional Timeline

| Period | Key Developments | Connections |
|---|---|---|
| **Deep history** (300,000+ YA) | Human origins; migration out of Africa; earliest tools and art | Africa is where all human history begins |
| **Neolithic/Iron Age** (3000 BCE-500 CE) | Bantu expansion; ironworking; agricultural intensification | Bantu migration spread languages, crops, and technology across the continent |
| **Classical kingdoms** (500-1500) | Ghana, Mali, Songhai (West Africa); Great Zimbabwe; Axum/Ethiopia; Swahili city-states | Trans-Saharan gold trade; Indian Ocean commerce; Islamic connections |
| **Slave trade era** (1500-1850) | Atlantic slave trade; internal transformations; Oyo, Dahomey, Asante | ~12.5 million enslaved people transported; demographic catastrophe |
| **Colonial period** (1884-1960s) | Berlin Conference partition; colonial extraction; resistance movements | Arbitrary borders; extractive institutions; racial ideologies |
| **Independence** (1960s-present) | National liberation; Cold War proxy conflicts; structural adjustment; contemporary growth | State-building with colonial institutional inheritance |
