# Everyday Life And Material Culture — Quick Reference


## Material Culture Categories

| Category | What to Examine | What It Reveals |
|---|---|---|
| **Food** | Diet, preparation, meal customs, famine frequency | Nutrition, class structure, trade networks, agricultural systems |
| **Clothing** | Fabric, style, sumptuary laws, production | Social hierarchy, trade, technology, gender norms |
| **Housing** | Structure, materials, layout, urban/rural | Family structure, class, technology, privacy norms |
| **Health** | Disease environment, medicine, life expectancy, childbirth | Knowledge systems, social investment, vulnerability |
| **Work** | Daily routine, seasonal cycles, tools, skills | Economic system, gender division of labor, technology |
| **Death** | Burial practices, mourning customs, life expectancy | Beliefs, social bonds, disease environment, inequality |
| **Leisure** | Festivals, sports, music, storytelling, religion | Values, social bonds, resistance, identity |
