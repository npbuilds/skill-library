# Environmental History — Quick Reference


## Key Climate Episodes

| Episode | Period | Historical Impact |
|---|---|---|
| **Late Bronze Age drought** | ~1200 BCE | Contributed to the collapse of Eastern Mediterranean palace civilizations |
| **Roman Climate Optimum** | 200 BCE – 200 CE | Warm, stable climate supported Roman agricultural expansion |
| **Medieval Warm Period** | ~900 – 1300 | Viking expansion to Greenland; European agricultural growth; Song Dynasty prosperity |
| **Little Ice Age** | ~1300 – 1850 | Crop failures, famine, social unrest; contributed to the General Crisis of the 17th century |
| **ENSO events** | Recurring | El Nino-driven droughts contributed to Indian famines under British rule (Mike Davis, *Late Victorian Holocausts*) |
