# Nexus Event Analyzer — Quick Reference


## Quick Reference

| Wing | What it contributes to understanding this event |
|---|---|
| Political | Power structures, governance changes, revolutions |
| Economic | Material conditions, trade, production, distribution |
| Cultural | Lived experience, identity, religion, social movements |
| Military | Conflict, technology, strategy, consequences of violence |
| Intellectual | Ideas that drove or resulted from the event |
| World-systems | Structural forces, environmental factors, demographic shifts |
