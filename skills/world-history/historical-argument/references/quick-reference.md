# Historical Argument — Quick Reference


## Quick Reference

| Framework | Core Question | Example Application |
|---|---|---|
| **Political/institutional** | How do power structures shape outcomes? | Why did democratic Athens invade Sicily? |
| **Economic/materialist** | How do material conditions drive change? | Why did the Industrial Revolution start in Britain? |
| **Cultural/ideational** | How do ideas, beliefs, and values shape action? | Why did the Reformation spread where it did? |
| **Social** | How do class, gender, race, and status structures matter? | Why did the French Revolution radicalize? |
| **Structural/systemic** | What deep forces operate beneath the surface? | Why do empires follow similar life cycles? |
| **Contingent/agentic** | How did specific decisions by specific people matter? | Would WWII have happened without Hitler? |
