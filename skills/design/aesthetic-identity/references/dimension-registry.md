# Dimension Registry

The canonical list of active aesthetic dimensions. Each dimension is a spectrum with two named poles, a current position (0.0–1.0), a confidence score (0.0–1.0), and a data point count.

Dimensions are grouped by category. New dimensions discovered by the observer are appended to the appropriate category or to "Discovered" if they don't fit.

---

## Spatial

### Density
**Sparse/Minimal** ◄────────────────► **Rich/Layered**
- Position: 0.68 | Confidence: 0.30 | Data points: 2
- What it measures: How much visual information occupies the canvas. Few elements with generous whitespace vs. many elements creating texture and complexity.
- Evidence: Archon dashboard — dense data presentation with gauges, tables, sector heat maps, yield curves, SVG charts. Accepted and praised without density complaints. Cosmic flame artwork — sparse void with concentrated energy mass. User absorbed this as valid alongside dense dashboard layouts. Suggests bimodal density preference: rich for operational, sparse-concentrated for contemplative.

### Symmetry
**Symmetric/Balanced** ◄────────────────► **Asymmetric/Dynamic**
- Position: 0.40 | Confidence: 0.23 | Data points: 1
- What it measures: Whether compositions favor equilibrium or deliberate imbalance. Centered, mirrored arrangements vs. off-axis, tension-creating layouts.
- Evidence: Grid-based dashboard layout with symmetric gauge row and balanced card columns. Slightly structured/ordered preference.

### Depth
**Flat/Planar** ◄────────────────► **Dimensional/Layered**
- Position: 0.62 | Confidence: 0.30 | Data points: 2
- What it measures: Use of visual depth cues — shadows, overlapping planes, parallax, blur, z-axis layering vs. single-plane, flat design.
- Evidence: Subtle panel layering, CRT vignette overlay, gauge ring depth, but not heavy shadow/parallax. Moderate depth. Cosmic flame artwork — extreme atmospheric depth via color layering, luminous bloom effects, and figure-ground scale differential. Deeper than dashboard work.

## Chromatic

### Temperature
**Cool** ◄────────────────► **Warm**
- Position: 0.38 | Confidence: 0.30 | Data points: 2
- What it measures: Dominant color temperature across outputs. Blues/greens/violets vs. reds/oranges/ambers/earth tones.
- Evidence: Light mode palette is blue-gray backgrounds (#e8ecf2), deep teal accents (#1a7070). Cool-dominant with warm accent pops (amber, red used for data signaling only). Cosmic flame artwork — warm-dominant (red/orange/magenta core) accepted for sublime/cosmic contexts. Cool remains default for operational work. Bimodal.

### Chromatic Range
**Monochromatic** ◄────────────────► **Polychromatic**
- Position: 0.65 | Confidence: 0.30 | Data points: 2
- What it measures: How many distinct hues appear in a typical output. Single-hue with value variation vs. full-spectrum color use.
- Evidence: Multi-hue but restrained — teal, amber, magenta, red, green all present but muted and purposeful (each color signals different data categories). Cosmic flame artwork — full spectrum within the flame form (red through orange through magenta through blue-violet). More polychromatic than dashboard palette but still purposeful, not chaotic.

### Contrast
**Subtle/Muted** ◄────────────────► **Bold/High-Impact**
- Position: 0.45 | Confidence: 0.30 | Data points: 2
- What it measures: Value contrast and saturation levels. Soft, close-valued palettes vs. dramatic light/dark and vivid saturation.
- Evidence: "Faded neon" was the explicit brief. Light mode uses muted, desaturated accent colors against soft backgrounds. User praised this. Not washed out, but deliberately restrained. Cosmic flame artwork — maximum contrast (pure white silhouette against pure black void, saturated flame mass). Accepted as valid alongside muted/faded neon. Context-dependent: high contrast for sublime, muted for operational.

## Form

### Geometry
**Geometric/Rectilinear** ◄────────────────► **Organic/Flowing**
- Position: 0.28 | Confidence: 0.30 | Data points: 2
- What it measures: Shape language. Hard edges, straight lines, circles/rectangles vs. freeform curves, natural forms, irregular boundaries.
- Evidence: Dashboard uses grid layout, rectangular cards, circular gauge rings, straight-line charts, bar charts. Strongly geometric vocabulary. Cosmic flame artwork — entirely organic: gestural flame strokes, flowing energy forms, no straight lines. Absorbed as counterweight to geometric default. Organic forms now part of vocabulary for contemplative/creative contexts.

### Precision
**Mechanical/Exact** ◄────────────────► **Expressive/Handmade**
- Position: 0.22 | Confidence: 0.30 | Data points: 2
- What it measures: Finish quality. Pixel-perfect, machine-made feel vs. visible hand, intentional imperfection, textures that suggest physical media.
- Evidence: Monospace typography (Share Tech Mono, Orbitron), pixel-precise alignment, consistent spacing tokens, data-table precision. Very mechanical. Cosmic flame artwork — maximally expressive. Visible brushstrokes, irregular edges, hand-directed energy. Accepted alongside mechanical precision. Suggests bimodal: mechanical for tools, expressive for creative/worldbuilding.

## Temporal

### Motion Feel
**Static/Calm** ◄────────────────► **Kinetic/Energetic**
- Position: 0.55 | Confidence: 0.30 | Data points: 2
- What it measures: Presence and intensity of animation and motion. Still compositions vs. persistent movement, particles, transitions, animated state.
- Evidence: Subtle animation — header line pulse, CSS digital rain, logo flicker. Present but understated. User praised the overall design including these subtle motions. Cosmic flame artwork — implied kinetic energy through directional brushwork and flame dynamics. Static image but reads as frozen motion. Suggests appetite for more dynamic motion than dashboard's subtle pulses.

### Temporal Register
**Retro/Classic** ◄────────────────► **Futuristic/Novel**
- Position: 0.55 | Confidence: 0.30 | Data points: 2
- What it measures: The era the aesthetic evokes. Vintage palettes, established patterns, nostalgia vs. bleeding-edge techniques, speculative forms, unfamiliar materiality.
- Evidence: User explicitly requested "cyberpunk but lived in. Vintage feel." CRT scanlines + Orbitron font = retro-futuristic blend. Not purely retro, not purely futuristic — a synthesis. Cosmic flame artwork — timeless rather than era-specific. Neither retro nor futuristic — mythic. Consistent with retro-futuristic blend but adds a "timeless/mythic" register.

## Emotional

### Emotional Register
**Serious/Austere** ◄────────────────► **Playful/Whimsical**
- Position: 0.25 | Confidence: 0.35 | Data points: 2
- What it measures: The emotional tone conveyed by visual choices. Formal, restrained, dignified vs. lighthearted, surprising, delightful.
- Evidence: Investment intelligence dashboard — serious data presentation with atmospheric styling. Not austere (has personality), but firmly on the serious side. Cosmic flame artwork — serious/sublime. Reinforces the serious pole with a new shade: not austere, not data-serious, but *awe*-serious. Strongest confirmation of this dimension.

### Information Stance
**Data-Forward** ◄────────────────► **Atmosphere-Forward**
- Position: 0.45 | Confidence: 0.30 | Data points: 2
- What it measures: Priority balance between conveying information and creating a feeling. Clarity and legibility first vs. mood and immersion first.
- Evidence: Dashboard is data-first (14 API feeds, tables, charts, metrics) but wrapped in strong atmospheric cyberpunk theming. Data leads, atmosphere supports. Cosmic flame artwork — fully atmosphere-forward. No data, pure mood. Accepted alongside data-forward dashboard. Bimodal: data-first for operational, atmosphere-first for creative.

## Discovered

### Light/Dark Preference
**Light Backgrounds** ◄────────────────► **Dark Backgrounds**
- Position: 0.35 | Confidence: 0.30 | Data points: 2
- What it measures: Preference for background luminance. Light, airy backgrounds vs. dark, immersive backgrounds.
- Discovery evidence: User was presented with both dark cyberpunk and light mode. Explicitly praised light mode: "i really love the light background design and theme and colors and everything." Cosmic flame artwork — deep black background. User absorbed this willingly. Light mode remains preferred for operational/dashboard contexts, but dark backgrounds are now valid for atmospheric/sublime work. Bimodal.
- Discovered: 2026-03-28

### Sublime Scale
**Intimate/Human-Scale** ◄────────────────► **Cosmic/Sublime**
- Position: 0.75 | Confidence: 0.30 | Data points: 1
- What it measures: Appetite for awe-scale imagery — vast cosmic spaces, overwhelming forces, the numinous. Whether compositions feel contained and personal or infinite and overwhelming.
- Discovery evidence: User engaged with cosmic flame artwork featuring a lone figure dwarfed by a massive energy form in a void. Immediately connected it to Cosmere, Cowboy Bebop gate sequences, and requested absorption. The sublime is a distinct aesthetic appetite.
- Discovered: 2026-04-15
