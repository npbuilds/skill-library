# Cultural Color Meanings

Color associations vary significantly across cultures. Always consider the target audience's cultural context.

## Red

| Region | Association |
|--------|------------|
| Western | Danger, passion, love, urgency, stop |
| East Asian | Luck, prosperity, celebration, joy (dominant wedding color in China) |
| South Asian | Purity, fertility, marriage (bridal color in India) |
| Middle Eastern | Danger, caution (similar to Western) |
| South African | Mourning, sacrifice |

## White

| Region | Association |
|--------|------------|
| Western | Purity, cleanliness, weddings, peace |
| East Asian | Death, mourning, funerals (China, Japan, Korea) |
| South Asian | Mourning, widowhood (traditional India) |
| Middle Eastern | Purity, peace (similar to Western) |

## Blue

| Region | Association |
|--------|------------|
| Western | Trust, calm, corporate, masculinity |
| East Asian | Immortality, healing (China); everyday/common (Japan) |
| Middle Eastern | Protection, spirituality, heaven |
| Latin American | Trust, serenity, mourning (some regions) |

## Yellow

| Region | Association |
|--------|------------|
| Western | Optimism, caution, warmth, cowardice |
| East Asian | Royalty, imperial power (China); courage (Japan) |
| Middle Eastern | Happiness, prosperity |
| Latin American | Death, mourning (some regions) |

## Green

| Region | Association |
|--------|------------|
| Western | Nature, growth, money, go/success |
| Islamic cultures | Sacred, paradise, the Prophet — use with particular respect |
| East Asian | Eternity, health, fertility |
| South American | Death (some regions) |

## Purple

| Region | Association |
|--------|------------|
| Western | Royalty, luxury, creativity, mystery |
| East Asian | Wealth, nobility (similar to Western) |
| South American | Mourning (Brazil) |
| Middle Eastern | Wealth, spirituality |

## Black

| Region | Association |
|--------|------------|
| Western | Sophistication, death, formality, power |
| East Asian | Masculinity, health, prosperity (China); formality (Japan) |
| Middle Eastern | Mystery, evil, rebirth |
| South Asian | Evil, negativity, darkness |

## Practical Guidance

- **International products**: Default to blue (most universally positive) with neutral greys
- **Regional targeting**: Research specific market — a color that signals "luxury" in one market may signal "mourning" in another
- **Semantic colors**: Success/error/warning meanings vary — always pair with icons or text, never color alone
- **Religious sensitivity**: Green in Islamic contexts and saffron/orange in Hindu/Buddhist contexts carry spiritual weight — use respectfully and intentionally
