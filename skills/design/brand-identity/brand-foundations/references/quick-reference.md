# Brand Foundations — Quick Reference


## Quick Reference

| Common Axis Pairs | When to Use |
|---|---|
| Premium ↔ Accessible | Price-sensitive markets |
| Traditional ↔ Innovative | Established categories with disruptors |
| Specialist ↔ Generalist | Professional/expert audiences |
| Playful ↔ Serious | Consumer-facing, tone-dependent brands |
| Personal ↔ Corporate | Service-based, trust-dependent brands |

## Quick Reference

| Layer | What It Captures | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Demographics | Who they are | 28-45, urban, $60-120K income |
| Psychographics | What they value | Efficiency, aesthetics, sustainability |
| Behavioral | What they do | Research extensively before buying, prefer self-service |
| Contextual | When/where they engage | Mobile-first, during commute, weekend projects |

## Personality to Execution Translation

| Personality Trait | Visual Implication | Verbal Implication |
|---|---|---|
| High sincerity | Warm colors, rounded shapes, natural imagery | Conversational, first-person, inclusive |
| High excitement | Bold colors, dynamic angles, motion | Short sentences, exclamation energy, action verbs |
| High competence | Clean layout, structured grids, data-forward | Precise language, evidence-based, authoritative |
| High sophistication | Restrained palette, generous whitespace, serif type | Formal register, curated vocabulary, understated |
| High ruggedness | Textured, earthy, high contrast | Direct, no-frills, imperative mood |

## Differentiation Types

| Type | Durability | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Feature | Low — easily copied | "We have dark mode" |
| Experience | Medium — hard to replicate at scale | "Our onboarding takes 30 seconds" |
| Values/Mission | High — requires cultural shift to copy | "We donate 1% of revenue to climate" |
| Audience Intimacy | High — built over time | "We know freelance designers because we are freelance designers" |
