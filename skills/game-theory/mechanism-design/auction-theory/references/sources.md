# Sources — Auction Theory

## Primary Texts

**Krishna, V. *Auction Theory* (Academic Press, 2nd ed. 2010)**
The definitive textbook. Covers IPV, common values, affiliated values, multi-unit auctions, mechanism design connections, and combinatorial auctions. Self-contained proofs throughout.
→ Used for: all standard auction theory, revenue equivalence, optimal auctions, multi-unit extensions.

**Milgrom, P. *Putting Auction Theory to Work* (Cambridge, 2004)**
Theory-to-practice bridge by one of the architects of the FCC spectrum auctions. Covers package auctions, activity rules, and practical mechanism design challenges.
→ Used for: SMR auction design, practical considerations, real-world auction applications.

**Milgrom, P. *Discovering Prices* (Columbia, 2017)**
Focuses on the Incentive Auction that repurposed TV spectrum for wireless broadband. Documents the design of the world's first two-sided spectrum auction.
→ Used for: modern auction design innovations, incentive auction case study.

## Key Papers — Foundational

**Vickrey, W. (1961)** "Counterspeculation, Auctions, and Competitive Sealed Tenders." *Journal of Finance* 16: 8-37.
→ Introduces the second-price sealed-bid auction and proves truthful bidding is dominant. The foundational paper of auction theory.

**Myerson, R.B. (1981)** "Optimal Auction Design." *Mathematics of Operations Research* 6: 58-73.
→ Characterizes revenue-maximizing auctions. Introduces virtual valuations and the optimal reserve price. One of the most cited papers in mechanism design.

**Riley, J.G. & Samuelson, W.F. (1981)** "Optimal Auctions." *American Economic Review* 71: 381-392.
→ Independent derivation of revenue equivalence and optimal auctions. Complementary to Myerson's approach.

**Milgrom, P.R. & Weber, R.J. (1982)** "A Theory of Auctions and Competitive Bidding." *Econometrica* 50: 1089-1122.
→ Introduces affiliated values model. Proves revenue ranking: English > second-price > first-price under affiliation. The linkage principle.

**Myerson, R.B. & Satterthwaite, M.A. (1983)** "Efficient Mechanisms for Bilateral Trading." *Journal of Economic Theory* 29: 265-281.
→ Impossibility result: no mechanism achieves efficiency, incentive compatibility, individual rationality, and budget balance simultaneously in bilateral trade.

## Key Papers — Multi-Item and Combinatorial

**Clarke, E.H. (1971)** "Multipart Pricing of Public Goods." *Public Choice* 11: 17-33.
**Groves, T. (1973)** "Incentives in Teams." *Econometrica* 41: 617-631.
→ Together with Vickrey (1961), form the VCG mechanism: dominant-strategy truthful, efficient allocation for multiple items.

**Ausubel, L.M. & Milgrom, P. (2002)** "Ascending Auctions with Package Bidding." *Frontiers of Theoretical Economics* 1: Article 1.
→ Ascending proxy auction for combinatorial settings. Core-selecting outcomes when goods are substitutes.

**Edelman, B., Ostrovsky, M. & Schwarz, M. (2007)** "Internet Advertising and the Generalized Second-Price Auction." *American Economic Review* 97: 242-259.
→ Analyzes GSP auctions used in search advertising. Nash equilibria correspond to stable matchings.

## Key Papers — Applied

**McAfee, R.P. & McMillan, J. (1987)** "Auctions and Bidding." *Journal of Economic Literature* 25: 699-738.
→ Classic survey. Accessible introduction to auction theory with practical applications.

**Cramton, P. (2013)** "Spectrum Auction Design." *Review of Industrial Organization* 42: 161-190.
→ Overview of spectrum auction design principles and FCC auction history.

**Athey, S. & Nekipelov, D. (2010)** "A Structural Model of Sponsored Search Advertising Auctions." Working paper.
→ Empirical analysis of search advertising auctions. Connects theory to observed bidding data.

## Nobel Prize Context

**2020 Nobel in Economics**: Paul Milgrom and Robert Wilson "for improvements to auction theory and inventions of new auction formats." Recognized their work on affiliated values, common-value auctions, and the practical design of the FCC spectrum auctions.
