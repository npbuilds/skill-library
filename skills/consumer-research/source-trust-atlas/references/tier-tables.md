# Trust Tier Tables — 2026 Snapshot

Operational roster behind the atlas tiers. Re-verify independence models during `deep` runs; refresh annually. Evidence base: `research/consumer-research-landscape-2026.md` §1.

## Tier 1 — Independent Testers

| Source | Scope | Independence model | Methodology | Known caveats |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Consumer Reports | Broad (appliances, cars, electronics, home) | Subscriptions; no ads; anonymous retail purchasing; no free samples | Published bulletins per category; lab tests + predicted reliability + owner satisfaction | Exact weighting formulas proprietary |
| Rtings | TVs, monitors, headphones, soundbars, peripherals | Subscriptions + disclosed revenue; no sponsored content | Versioned test benches (since 2019), fully published | 2024 TV scoring update contested by enthusiast forums |
| Which? (UK) | Broad, UK-centric | Subscriptions; no advertising | Simulated real-world durability (e.g., 30k barrel rolls ≈ 10y mattress wear) | UK market availability differs from US |
| America's Test Kitchen | Kitchen/cooking only | No free samples, no manufacturer trips, no display ads | Real-recipe comparative testing | Narrow scope |
| Project Farm (YouTube) | Tools, hardware, consumables, automotive | Self-funded purchases; declines sponsorships; viewer-supported | Comparative rigs, repeatable setups — but protocols not formally published | Single-operator; trust rests on track record |

## Tier 2 — Independent Editorial, Affiliate-Funded

| Source | Scope | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Wirecutter (NYT) | Broad | 20-40h research, 50-100 candidates → 10-15 finalists, weeks of real-world testing; affiliate revenue structural; rubrics unpublished |
| Good Housekeeping Institute | Home, kitchen, family | Multidisciplinary lab (engineering, culinary, chemistry); less methodology transparency |
| TechRadar / CNET | Electronics, software | Real reviews, variable update cadence — check test dates |

## Tier 3 — Crowd Sources (verify before weighting)

| Source | Value | Risk | Protocol |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reddit (product/hobby subs) | Long-term ownership reports, failure modes, niche expertise | Documented astroturfing campaigns (marketing firms posting "organic" comments at scale, 2024-2025); bots evade moderation | Check account age, history breadth, cross-thread repetition; prefer threads with disagreement; never count one thread as more than one source |
| YouTube reviewers (general) | Hands-on demonstrations | Sample-seeding and sponsorship hard to audit | Check disclosure, who paid for the unit, channel's correction history |
| Capterra / G2 (software) | Aggregate user sentiment, feature gaps | Vendor incentive programs skew volume | Weight verified, recent, detailed reviews; ignore star averages |

## Tier 4 — Adversarial (never evidence)

- Generic "best X <year>" affiliate SEO listicles — documented takeover of product search results; 71% of tracked affiliate domains penalized by the March 2026 Google core update, yet long-tail queries still surface them.
- AI content farms — ~80% month-over-month growth in AI-generated review content.
- Manufacturer/merchant copy and AI-SEO-optimized content (tools now exist specifically to influence LLM shopping recommendations) — treat as claims requiring triangulation, never as corroboration.

## Standing Signals

- **FTC consumer-review rule** (effective 2024-10-21, penalties to $51,744/violation): a seller or brand with documented enforcement history gets a standing red flag in any brief that touches it.
- **Disclosure quality**: transparent "how we make money" pages (Rtings model) upgrade confidence; hidden affiliate relationships downgrade a source one tier.
